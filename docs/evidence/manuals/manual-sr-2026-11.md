Heading1: 1. About this manual

This manual explains every screen of the ARON SR app in the order an SR uses it through the day: install and log in, check in, take stock, connect the printer, sell outlet by outlet, collect dues, and finally do Bikroy Joma (sales submit) and check out. Each step shows one screenshot (or two related screens) on the left, and on the right what the screen shows and what to do.

ListParagraph: Who uses the app: the SR (Sales Representative), a van seller who takes stock from the distributor house each morning and sells to the outlets of the day&apos;s route.

ListParagraph: Language: screens are in Bangla (English can be chosen in Settings). This manual gives the English meaning of every label; Annex A lists them.

ListParagraph: Common controls: back arrow (top left); outlet drop-down showing &quot;Outlet name (code-phone-cluster)&quot;; alphabet bar (All, A, B ...) to filter outlets by first letter; - / + boxes or a pop-up of quick buttons (+20, +100, -20 ...) to enter quantity.

ListParagraph: Units: Cigarette and Bidi in sticks, Lighter in pieces, Match in dozens. Prices on the Sales screen are per 1,000 sticks.

ListParagraph: Screenshots: from test and live accounts, so names and figures are examples. Every screenshot is numbered; the text describes everything on the screen.

Heading1: 25. Quick answers (FAQ)

Question

Answer

The app asks for an OTP.

First login on a device or after a new version: ask your TSO for the 4-digit OTP (section 3).

What is the order of the day?

Check in &gt; stock and printer &gt; sell outlet by outlet &gt; collect dues &gt; Bikroy Joma &gt; check out (after 5 PM).

How do I start selling at an outlet?

Sales &gt; choose outlet &gt; হ্যাঁ to start the call &gt; outlet photo &gt; enter quantities &gt; এগিয়ে যান &gt; review &gt; প্রিন্ট.

It says I am not within the retailer&apos;s range.

Tap রিফ্রেশ. If you are at the outlet, tap ফোর্স সেল and choose Internet problem or Location change.

The outlet did not buy.

Do a zero sale: start the call, tap এগিয়ে যান with no SKU, confirm and choose the reason.

The retailer pays later.

Tick &quot;mark as due&quot; on the review and enter the amount paid now; collect later in Memo &gt; পরিশোধিত করুন.

How do I return faulty sticks?

Review &gt; প্রোডাক্ট QC &gt; choose SKU &gt; enter fault quantity &gt; QC জমা দিন.

How do I fix a wrong sale?

Memo &gt; outlet &gt; এডিট &gt; choose the reason &gt; correct &gt; print. Only inside the geo-fence and not after QC.

How do I collect empty packs?

Collect DRP Discount on the Sales screen &gt; enter slides &gt; জমা দিন.

How do I add or close an outlet?

Outlet &gt; নতুন দোকান or স্থায়ী বন্ধ; the AMO verifies it.

How do I see my targets?

Dashboard &gt; Target &amp; Achievement &gt; বিস্তারিত; KPI Dashboard; Today&apos;s target at each outlet.

How do I finish the day?

Bikroy Joma: ডাটা সিঙ্ক করুন, check Device = Server, বিক্রয় জমা; then check out.

Can I sell after Bikroy Joma?

No.
