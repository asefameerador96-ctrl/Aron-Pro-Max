# PROPOSAL (D-TKPI-14, lead's ruling after the review of c6c799b69): EXACT BY IDENTITY replaces the inference rule below

Status: SPIKE built and tested on `lane/sr-targets-server` (`SrFigureProbe`, `BundleService.bundle(..., probe)`, the `id*` tests of `SrPhoneWouldShowTest`); the contract text and the transport are NOT changed yet (the inference text further down is still the one in `contract/openapi.yaml` until the lead approves this proposal). Three review rounds each found new cases because the phone INFERS from acknowledgements, resolutions, quarantine states and ordering what the server figure contains. The proposal removes the inference: the phone ASKS, by uuid, and the server answers in the same snapshot as the figures.

## The exchange
With its request for figures the phone sends a probe list: for each of its own memos in the bundle month whose status in the figure it does not know (never confirmed IN, pending, sent and unanswered, held, refused, or that it has voided or superseded locally) `{memo_client_uuid, memo_no, business_date, removed_locally}`; at most 300 (see the limits). The server answers, computed inside the SAME repeatable-read transaction as the figures:
- `in_figure`: the memo is stored, active, `line_count > 0`, not voided, has at least one non-voided line, and is dated from the 1st of the month up to `through_date`; with `lines` = the number of its stored lines (the phone sees when a line was refused for good, e.g. a zero price) and, when `removed_locally`, the stored lines (sku, base quantity, kind) so the phone subtracts exactly what the server holds;
- `voided`: the server holds it and it is gone for good (voided, superseded, admin data void, every line voided);
- anything not listed is NOT IN: not stored (never uploaded, in flight and not committed at the snapshot, held for review, refused) or stored but dated after `through_date`.
A uuid that is not the caller's own answers NOT IN and reveals nothing. PAPER TWIN: when the uuid is unknown and an own paper-backfill memo (`entry_source = manual`) with the same `memo_no` and business date exists (the number is unique per date; the existing twin is identified by exactly that), the answer is the twin's.

## The phone rule (no acknowledgements, resolutions, quarantine states, clocks or single flight)
For each own memo m of the month, live or removed on the phone:
1. dated after `through_date`: the figure never holds it; ADD it if it is live and not `voided`; a `voided` answer (admin data void of today) drops it;
2. dated up to `through_date`, live on the phone: `in_figure` -> add nothing (remember "confirmed IN", do not probe it again until the phone itself voids or edits it); `voided` -> drop it, add nothing; NOT IN -> ADD it;
3. dated up to `through_date`, voided or superseded on the phone: `in_figure` -> SUBTRACT the stored lines the answer lists; otherwise nothing;
4. a memo confirmed IN and later voided by the office leaves the figure by itself; the phone adds nothing for it (the office's truth).
A phone that sends no probe (old app) gets the server figure alone, shows it with its as-of label, adds only memos dated after `through_date` and its never-sent pending ones.

## Invariant and proof
Let S be the one snapshot, F(S) the figure, A(S) the answer for the probe set P. For an own memo m define c(m) its contribution (quantities of its sale lines, counts per the contract definitions). CLAIM: the phone shows T(S) = sum of c(m) over every memo that is live in S (stored active memos of the caller dated in the month, other users' memos at a shared outlet for the campaign figure) plus the phone's live memos that S does not hold, counted once each. PROOF: F(S) = sum of c over the stored active memos dated in the month up to `through_date` (by the server query, in S). Every own memo is in exactly one class in S, decided by S itself: (a) IN: counted in F, the phone adds nothing, counted once; if the phone removed it, the subtraction is of the stored lines, which are exactly what F counted; (b) VOIDED: not in F, the phone adds nothing, counted zero times, as the server truth; (c) NOT IN and dated up to `through_date`: not in F (by the definition of the answer), the phone adds its local contribution, once; (d) dated after `through_date`: not in F (cut), the phone adds, once, unless VOIDED. A memo committing after S is NOT IN in S and is added: exact for that bundle, and IN for the next. A memo confirmed IN earlier and untouched stays in F unless the server removed it (void or supersede, the only ways: nothing changes a stored memo's route, outlet or date), in which case F lacks it and the phone adds nothing: T(S) of the server. The answer and F are read in one repeatable-read transaction, so no interleaving of commits, rebuilds or releases can make A(S) disagree with F(S) (`aMemoCommittedWhileTheBundleIsReadIsAnsweredInExactlyWhenTheFigureHoldsIt` asserts that every build: memos answered IN = the figure's quantity). HOLES of the reviews and why they close: a held or released void or edit (the old memo is IN until the server really voids it, then VOIDED: never subtracted twice; the edit is NOT IN until stored: never lost); a line refused for good (the memo is IN with `lines` = 1: the phone adds nothing, nothing doubles on every bundle); a held memo of today released before the phone hears (today is cut: added; yesterday's: IN: not added); admin data void of today (VOIDED: dropped); the paper twin (IN through the memo number); a lost response (the memo is IN or NOT IN as committed, no ack involved); a bundle taken while a batch is in flight (the memo is NOT IN or IN as committed at S, no single flight needed).
LIMITS, stated: (1) the probe is capped; a phone with more unconfirmed memos in the window than the cap cannot get an exact total in one request, it shows "syncing" until its uploads and confirmations bring it under the cap; (2) the paper-twin answer trusts the memo number as the identity the existing backfill review uses (a reviewer can still find the two differ, then they are two memos); (3) the other SRs' memos at a shared outlet are in the campaign figure by design.

## Cost and size
Lookup of at most 300 memos by `(client_uuid, business_date)` (the unique index, dates given so only the month's partitions are touched) plus one stored-line read of those memos by the `memo_line_no` index, inside the figures' snapshot; the paper-twin lookup only for unknown uuids by `(memo_no, business_date)`. Measured on the scratch set (`SrBundleScaleMeasureTest`, numbers in `docs/status/sr-targets-server.md`). Request about 70 bytes per entry, answer about 50 bytes per IN memo plus the voided list and, for removed memos only, their lines.

## Transport (proposal, additive)
Per-caller answers cannot live in the bundle: its version (ETag, 304, delta) is a content digest of the bundle. Proposed: `POST /v1/sync/figures` with `{for?, probe: [...]}` returning, per route of the caller, the figure sections (`targets`, `achievement_mtd`, `calendar`, `kpi`, `star`, `campaigns` with their flags) plus `figure_probe`, built by the same loaders in ONE repeatable-read transaction, without the 290 KB bundle. The bundle keeps carrying the same sections (old phones). The Astha section is built on a handle inside a repeatable-read transaction already and is a live snapshot through today; it can answer from the same probe list by running in the SAME transaction as the figures with its own IN predicate (window to today, sale memos, no active edit); its redemption figures need the same identity for redemption uuids (loyalty lane). The whole bundle in one transaction is not needed for exactness.

# SUPERSEDED BY THE PROPOSAL ABOVE once approved: the inference rule as currently in the contract


Status: the server side (rule A) is BUILT on `lane/sr-targets-server` (D-TKPI-12): own-route figures read straight from the memo tables in one repeatable-read snapshot; tests of what the phone would show in `SrPhoneWouldShowTest` (a model of this rule) and `SrPhoneWouldShowIngestTest` (the same through the real ingest, quarantine release, accept-with-fix and the waiting sweep). The binding text is the `RouteSnapshot` description in `contract/openapi.yaml`; this file says the same in the phone lane's terms, and if the two ever differ the contract wins. NOTHING here reads a timestamp: `as_of` is informational and a phone must never compare it with a clock.

## What the server figure is
For `achievement_mtd`, `kpi.*` (volumes, memo counts, `by_pack`), `star` STD and `campaigns[].std/memo/skus`: all accepted memos (status active, lines not voided) with a business date from the 1st of the bundle `month` up to and including `through_date` (bundle date minus one day), read in ONE repeatable-read snapshot from `app.memo` / `app.memo_line`. It holds every memo the server had COMMITTED when it read, whether the phone has heard of it or not. The bundle date's own memos are never in it. (A bundle built ahead for tomorrow has `through_date` = the day it was built on, so that day's committed memos ARE in it; the rule still gives the true total.)

Definitions, the same for the phone's local additions:
- quantities (`achieved_qty_base`, brand and category `volume_*`, `by_pack`, Star STD, campaign STD, campaign SKU `sold_qty_base`): SALE lines only;
- `achievement_mtd.memo_count`: a memo counts once per SKU when it has ANY non-voided line of that SKU (free lines included; a free-only memo gives `achieved_qty_base` 0 and `memo_count` 1);
- KPI brand `memo_achieved` and campaign `memo`: a memo counts once when it has a SALE line of the brand (campaign: of a shown campaign SKU);
- brand sums use ONE hierarchy, the current product tree, for target and figure;
- campaign figures are the OUTLET's: every route's memos at the outlet committed before the read, through `through_date`, plus this phone's own local memos. Another SR's sales at a shared outlet are in the figure; that SR's unsynced and today's memos are not.

## The phone rule (ordering only)
Let `R` be the moment the phone SENT the request for this bundle (its own sync-cycle counter).
1. A memo is ACKNOWLEDGED when the phone holds `accepted` or `duplicate` for the memo AND for all its lines, or a resolution `accepted` / `accepted_with_fix` for it (from a batch response or a delta), received before `R`. The server commits a family before it returns its ack (`IngestService.runFamilies`), so the server figure holds every memo acknowledged before `R`.
2. A memo whose last answer is `quarantined` (held for review, or waiting for a parent record) is NEVER added: show it as held for review (the awaiting line); it is not a counted sale yet and the server figure lacks it. The server may commit it before the phone hears (a reviewer's accept or accept_with_fix, or the sweep of waiting children): then the figure holds it and the phone, which still does not add it, is exact. It becomes acknowledged only when an acknowledgement or resolution reaches the phone before a bundle request. A resolution `rejected` (or any final refusal) removes it; never add it.
3. ADD a live local memo dated inside the bundle `month` iff it is NOT acknowledged before `R` and not held or refused; ADD every such local memo dated after `through_date`, acknowledged or not (the figure is cut there).
4. SUBTRACT a memo acknowledged before `R` and dated up to `through_date` when the phone has since voided or superseded it and that void or edit record was not acknowledged before `R`. An edit is: add the new memo, subtract the old one.
5. SINGLE FLIGHT: send NO bundle request while an upload batch is in flight. A batch whose response never arrived (lost response, timeout, a 5xx after the server committed) is still in flight: replay it until it is answered (the answer is `duplicate`), then ask.
6. A bundle is merged with the memos as they stood at its own `R`; the next bundle applies the rule again with its new `R`.
7. An admin data void with no phone event takes memos out of the figure: until the phone learns of the void its total can be below its local memos. Correct for the business; nothing to do.

## Star
The phone merges the Star STD by rules 1-4 like every other figure and recomputes projection, achievement, slab and status with the shared `StarProgress` code. Last month's sold quantity (`lms`) is not merged (a last-day memo of last month not yet acknowledged is missing from it).

## Day counts when today's memos are in the achieved figure
ADS, PADS and TADS divide by `calendar.elapsed_working_days_before_today` plus 1 when `calendar.today_is_working` (default until Q-A), RADS by `calendar.remaining_working_days_after_today`; a zero divisor gives no figure.

## Tests the phone lane needs (what the phone would show)
Two offline days synced and a bundle pulled at every interleaving; an edit with identical lines; a void then a re-sale; a bundle right after an upload ack; a memo quarantined then released (before and after the phone hears); accept_with_fix; the waiting sweep; a free-only memo; a bundle on the 1st; a prefetched bundle; two routes. Each must show exactly the true total, never less, never more. The server-side twins are `SrPhoneWouldShowTest` and `SrPhoneWouldShowIngestTest`.
