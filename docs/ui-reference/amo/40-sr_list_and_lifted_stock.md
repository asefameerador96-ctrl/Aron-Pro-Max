## Navigation and behaviour
- Per the manual: tap SR Stock on the Dashboard, then tap an SR in the list to see what he lifted today.
- Tapping a row (or its chevron) on the left screen opens "লিফটেড স্টক" for that SR (right screen), showing the SR header and the SKU table with issue quantity (today's lifted stock).
- Back arrow returns to the previous screen (from lifted stock to the SR list, from the list to the Dashboard).
- The right-screen header card is not tappable (no chevron).
