## Data and figures shown
- Each row = achievement / target for that product category in the period, then achievement as a percentage of target. Target on the right-hand screen is the "till date" share of the monthly target (the part due up to today), so it is smaller than the monthly target (e.g. cigarette zone target 2,270,000 monthly vs 1,828,551 till date); the achievement is the same on both screens (1,598,680).
- Order of numbers on a row: achievement first, then target (left to right: "১,৫৯৮,৬৮০/২,২৭০,০০০").
- Zone card = whole zone (Dariapur); route cards = each route of the zone (Math Bazer, Dariapur, and more below by scrolling).
- Only Cigarette, Lighter, Match rows appear on this zone/routes (no Bidi row here; the manual says "and Bidi" where applicable, and screen 35 shows the বিড়ি row for another zone).
- Units are not shown on the screen. Cigarette figures are large (hundreds of thousands to millions: sticks), lighter and match figures are small (pieces or dozens); the manual does not state the unit. Figures use Bangla digits with Western thousands grouping (3-digit groups: ২,২৭০,০০০), not the lakh/crore grouping.
- The manual's sample "1,698,680 / 2,290,000 = 74%" differs from the digits in the image (see differences).

## Colours and style
- Top bar: deep purple (about #4A1A9A), white bold title, white back arrow. Solid, no gradient.
- Page background: very light lavender-white. Cards: white, large corner radius (about 24 px), soft shadow. The Zone card is tinted light grey-green to stand out.
- Tab toggle: pill/rounded rectangle with green outline; selected segment filled green (about #50BE80); unselected white.
- Progress bar: thin rounded bar, light grey track. Fill colour bands by percentage: red (below about 40%, e.g. 5, 30, 36, 39 and 36%), amber/yellow (about 40% to 79%, e.g. 45, 48, 55, 58, 70 and 70%), green (80% and above, e.g. 83, 87, 100%). The percentage text takes the same colour as its bar (red, amber, green). Band thresholds are inferred from the examples (red up to 39, amber 45 to 70, green from 83); exact cut-offs are not visible.
- Row label text dark; achievement/target figures dark grey, smaller; the "বিস্তারিত →" link black with arrow icon.
- No packshots, no gradient icons on this screen.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Back arrow returns to the dashboard.
- Two-segment toggle switches between Monthly target and Till Date Target; the whole list re-renders (same cards, different targets and colours).
- "বিস্তারিত →" on a card opens the brand detail for that zone/route (screen 35 shows the zone version, with a bar summary and an Item table).
- The list scrolls vertically through all routes.
- Manual says "Till Date Target = the part of the monthly target due up to today".

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The tab-1 label in the image is "মান্থলি লক্ষ্য" (transliteration of Monthly); the manual and Annex A write "মাসিক লক্ষ্য". Tab 2 is English "Till Date Target" in both.
- The manual's example "Cigarette 1,698,680 / 2,290,000 = 74%" does not match the visible digits (image reads about 1,598,680 / 2,270,000 = 70%). The manual figures are likely approximate or from another capture; use the image.
- Figures are in Bangla digits; the manual shows Latin digits.
- The manual does not mention the Zone card's different tint, the arrow on the details link, or that percentage text is coloured to match the bar.
- Heading format "Zone: Dariapur" is fully English, while route cards use Bangla "রুট:" with a Latin name; the manual does not mention this.
