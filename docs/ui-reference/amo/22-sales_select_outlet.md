## Layout, top to bottom
Left screen:
1. Purple strip at the top (status bar area only; the app bar is covered by the panel). A thin vertical scrollbar at the right edge of the list (grey, upper part).
2. A white full-width panel with shadow listing outlets, each row about 100 px tall: a solid green dot at the left (online/active indicator), optionally followed by a small round brown circular avatar/badge icon (a face-like emblem), then the outlet text "Name (code-phone-cluster)" in black.
3. First row "Jointo Store (...)" has a grey highlighted background (focused row). Rows with the extra brown emblem: Billal Store, M/S Yeasin Store, Sumon Store, Dilip Stor, M/S Mojid Store. Rows without it: all others. The emblem's meaning is not stated (see questions).
4. 14 rows visible; the list continues (scrollbar).
Right screen:
1. Purple top bar with back arrow and title "বিক্রয়".
2. Route field: white, rounded, purple outline, value "LaxmipurDaily" in black, down-arrow at right.
3. Retailer field below: white, purple outline, placeholder "রিটেইলার নির্বাচন করুন" in grey, down-arrow at right.
4. Alphabet filter bar: dark grey (#505050-like) rounded rectangle, two rows of letters in white: row 1 "All" (selected: green gradient rounded square highlight, white text), A, B, D, H, J, L, M, N, R, S; row 2 (centred) T, m, s. Letters are the first letters of outlets on the route (lower-case m and s appear as separate entries from capital M and S).
5. Sales tile (white rounded card; gradient pink-to-blue box+clock icon) with label "বিক্রয়" in purple.
6. Empty page below; home bar at bottom.

## Colours and style
- Top bar deep purple; page pale lavender; fields white with purple outline, rounded.

- Brown circular emblem: small, round, brown/orange with a stylised face.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Per manual: choose the outlet from the list, then tap বিক্রয় (the Sales tile) to proceed to the Sales screen (screenshots 23, 24).
- Tapping a letter filters the retailer list by initial; "All" shows all.

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual does not mention the green dot or the brown emblem beside outlets, the scrollbar, nor that the alphabet bar has letters A B D H J L M N R S T m s.
- The manual says the right screenshot shows "route and outlet selected"; in the image the retailer field is still the placeholder (outlet not yet chosen).
