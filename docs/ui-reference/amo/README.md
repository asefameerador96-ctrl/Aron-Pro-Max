Source: `ARON_AMO_Manual_Screenshots_JPG_1.zip` (77 JPEGs, one per numbered screenshot of the owner's AMO manual) and `ARON_AMO_App_User_Manual_2.docx`; the sanitised manual text is `docs/evidence/manuals/manual-amo-2026-10.md`. These are the **visual ground truth for the AMO app** (owner directive 14a: the screenshot wins over docs/32 and over the manual text). Next to every image `NN-<slug>.md` is its parity note (layout top to bottom, every visible text verbatim in Bangla and English, figures, colours, behaviour, differences from the manual text). `parts` = number of phone screens in the image (2 = left and right).

Binding rules that the screenshots do not override: units rule 5 (the phone enters, shows, prints and totals cigarettes in packets, lighters in boxes of 25, matches in dozens; the vendor app counts sticks), the docs/27 deferrals (Astha, every target/loyalty/discount/promotion programme including the "Campaign Enrollment" tile of screenshot 08: hooks only, no live data, no invented numbers), offline-first, idempotent sync, server-side scope, Bangla-first strings in the localisation layer.
