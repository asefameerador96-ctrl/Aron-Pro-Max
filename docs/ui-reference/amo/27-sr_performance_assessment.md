## Navigation and behaviour
Reached from Control Call menu > এসআর পারফ. অ্যাসেসমেন্ট after selecting route and outlet (manual section 8/17-20). Per the manual: in Distribution Performance tick every brand present at the outlet, even if it is out of stock at the moment of assessment; in OOS Performance tick the brands/SKUs the outlet sells but has no stock of right now; for POSM choose হ্যাঁ or না (poster/banner at the outlet). Tap সংরক্ষণ to save. On success a dialog "ডেটা সফলভাবে সংরক্ষিত হয়েছে" appears (screenshot 28). Back arrow returns to the Control Call menu. POSM appears to be a pair of checkboxes behaving as an exclusive choice (screenshot 28 shows only না ticked).

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual says "tick the SKUs" in the OOS list; the screen lists brands (18 brand names), not SKUs.
- The manual does not mention the instruction texts inside the two headers, nor the heading colours (orange for Distribution, purple for OOS).
- The manual lists the brands in the same order; the image confirms 18 brands. The manual writes "Existing Abul Bidi/42 No."; the image shows "Existing Abul Bidi/ 42 No." wrapped over lines (a space after the slash).
- The manual describes POSM as "হ্যাঁ or না"; image shows them as checkboxes (not radio buttons).
- Manual says nothing of a validation rule (e.g. is POSM required).
