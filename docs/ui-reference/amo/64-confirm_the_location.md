## Data and figures shown
- No app figures. The blue dot is the current GPS position; the light-blue circle is the accuracy radius; the map is centred on the user. The visible area is Banani, Dhaka (Kemal Ataturk Avenue).

## Colours and style
- Top bar deep purple, white title.
- Map: standard Google Maps light style; blue dot with light-blue accuracy circle; avatar marker (man in navy suit).
- Button: green gradient, white text, rounded, floating over the map.
- Compass and my-location buttons: white with grey icons.
- No packshots, no progress bars.

## Navigation and behaviour
Per the manual: check that the pin is on the outlet and tap নিশ্চিত করুন. The new base location becomes the outlet's geo-fence point. It should be done only at the outlet. (This follows the camera photo step after আপডেট in screenshot 63.) The my-location button recentres the map; the compass resets orientation (standard Google Maps behaviour; not stated in the manual). Back arrow returns to the previous screen.

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual says "Google Maps with your position and the button নিশ্চিত করুন". The image also shows a custom avatar marker, a blue accuracy circle, the compass and my-location buttons and extra pins, none mentioned in the manual.
- The manual does not say whether the pin can be dragged; the image shows no draggable pin, only the avatar on the blue dot.
