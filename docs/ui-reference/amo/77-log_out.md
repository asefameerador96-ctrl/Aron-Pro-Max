## Screens in the image

## Layout, top to bottom
1. Top bar: deep purple (very dark indigo-purple), full width, no gradient visible. At left a back arrow (outlined, rounded left-pointing triangle/chevron, light/white-grey). Title centred: "সেটিং" in light/white Bangla text. The bar is dimmed by the scrim but is the same deep purple.
2. Page background: light grey, dimmed to a mid grey (scrim darkens everything, about 40-50% black).
3. Row of three equal tiles directly under the top bar (white/very light rounded cards with faint shadow, side by side, dimmed):
   - Left tile: language selector. Black flag icon followed by "বাংলা" and a dark purple down-pointing triangle (drop-down). No circle icon. Text vertically centred in the tile.
   - Middle tile: circular gradient icon (pink/red to blue-purple) with a globe and a monitor/PC with up/down arrows (data transfer). Caption below in dark purple bold: "PDA টু সাপোর্ট".
   - Right tile: circular icon in purple/magenta gradient with a white power/exit-arrow glyph ("C" shape with arrow pointing right). Caption below in dark purple bold: "লগ আউট".
4. Centre of the screen: modal dialog, white, large rounded corners (about 24 px radius), strong dark soft shadow around it, width about 80 percent of screen, vertically at about 40 to 60 percent of screen height:
   a. Top centre: blue filled circle with a white lowercase "i" (information icon).
   b. Message, centred, blue (medium blue, like #1976D2) Bangla text: "আপনি কি নিশ্চিত যে লগআউট করতে চান?"
   c. Button row at the bottom right of the dialog, two buttons side by side with a small gap: green button "হ্যাঁ" (left, Yes) and red button "না" (right, No). Both small rounded rectangles with a light shadow, white bold text. Both enabled.
5. Below the dialog: empty dimmed grey page. Bottom: Android gesture bar (black strip with a short light line).

## Colours and style
- Top bar: deep purple / dark indigo (about #2A0F55 when dimmed); white title and back arrow.
- Page background: light grey (dimmed to mid grey by scrim).
- Tiles: white/light, rounded corners, very faint shadow; icons are circular gradient badges (pink-red to purple-blue for PDA support, purple to magenta for log out); captions dark purple bold.
- Scrim: black at roughly 40-50 percent over the whole screen including the top bar.
- Dialog: white card, large rounded corners, heavy soft shadow; info icon blue circle with white "i"; message text blue; Yes button green (about #4CAF50), No button red (about #F44336), white bold labels, small rounded corners, light shadow.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Reached from Home -> সেটিং (Settings) -> tap the লগ আউট tile.
- Tapping হ্যাঁ logs the user out (manual: "Tap লগ আউট, then হ্যাঁ"). Tapping না dismisses the dialog and stays on Settings (standard behaviour; the manual does not state it).
- Manual note: do Bikroy Joma (বিক্রয় জমা, sales submit) before logging out, so no data is left on the phone.
- The other Settings tiles (language drop-down, PDA টু সাপোর্ট) are described in section 23.
