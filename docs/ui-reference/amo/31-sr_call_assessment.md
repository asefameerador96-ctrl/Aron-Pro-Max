## Navigation and behaviour
- Reached by tapping the SR Call Assessment tile (screenshot 30). The AMO watches the SR's call and gives 1 to 5 stars for each of the seven items, then taps সংরক্ষণ (Save) at the bottom (screenshot 32), which saves and shows "ডেটা সফলভাবে সংরক্ষিত হয়েছে" and OK.
- Tapping a star sets the rating (1 = Low, 5 = উচ্চ). Back arrow leaves the screen.
- The whole page scrolls; header bar stays fixed.
