## Navigation and behaviour
- Per the manual: left list shows outlets that SRs asked to close permanently. Tap one to open it (right). Check the outlet. Tap সংরক্ষণ to approve the close or বাতিল to reject it, then confirm with হ্যাঁ in a confirmation dialog (not shown in this image; Annex A: "আপনি কি নিশ্চিত?").
- On success the "Data Updated Successfully" dialog appears (screenshot 51).
- Back arrow returns to the previous screen.
## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual does not mention the editable-looking route and cluster drop-downs on the right (they show the request's values), nor that shop name/owner/mobile are grey read-only text.
- Right screen shows its phone without a leading zero (same as the list).
- Button icons: Cancel has a circled X, Save a floppy-disk icon; not in the manual.
