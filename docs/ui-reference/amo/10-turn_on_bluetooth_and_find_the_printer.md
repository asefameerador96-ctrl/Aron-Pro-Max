# 10. Turn on Bluetooth and find the printer

## Screens in the image
- Left: Android (Samsung One UI, dark theme) system Settings > Bluetooth screen with Bluetooth switched off. Not the vendor app.
- Right: the same Settings > Bluetooth screen with Bluetooth on, scanning, listing the printer RPP02N under Available devices. Not the vendor app.
- Status bar: not shown.

## Layout, top to bottom
Left screen (Bluetooth off):
1. Black page. Top row: white back chevron "<" at left, title "Bluetooth" in white (handwriting-style system font), three-dot overflow menu at right.
2. A dark-grey rounded bar with the word "Off" in white at left and a toggle switch at right, in the off position (grey track, white knob on the left).
3. Below, grey helper text: "Turn on Bluetooth to connect to nearby devices."
4. Rest of page empty black; gesture bar line at the bottom.

Right screen (Bluetooth on):
1. Black page. Top row: back chevron, title "Bluetooth", text button "Stop" (white, scanning can be stopped) and a three-dot menu.
2. Dark-grey rounded bar with "On" in blue at left, a small multicolour (blue/green) dots scanning indicator, and the toggle in the on position (blue track, white knob on the right).
3. Grey helper text (three lines): "Make sure the device you want to connect to is in pairing mode. Your phone (MD's A12) is currently visible to nearby devices."
4. Small grey section header "Available devices".
5. One dark-grey rounded row: white printer icon at left, white text "RPP02N".
6. Gesture bar line at the bottom.

## Data and figures shown
- Printer name RPP02N (RPP02N-class 58 mm Bluetooth thermal printer). Phone name in the example: "MD's A12". No other figures.

## Colours and style
- Dark system theme: pure black background, dark-grey rounded rows, white text, blue for the "On" state and active toggle. Printer glyph white outline. Entirely Android system UI; no vendor styling.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Per the manual: switch on the printer (Power) and the phone's Bluetooth. The printer (e.g. "RPP02N") appears under Available devices. Tap the printer name to start pairing (next screenshot, 11).
- The toggle turns Bluetooth on; "Stop" ends scanning.
