## Data and figures shown
- LEFT: first card is the whole Zone (Dariapur) summed; then one card per route of the zone (Math Bazer, Dariapur, ...). Per category: target, sales, % = sales / target (e.g. 1,598,680 / 1,828,552.5 = 87.43 %), memo = number of memos. Targets carry decimals (".5", ".3", ".6") because they are prorated to date ("till now"). The left-screen % has no % sign; the right-screen % has one.
- Units: cigarette figures in sticks (large numbers), lighter and match in pieces or dozens (small numbers); the image does not state units.
- RIGHT: for route Motiharpol, CPR 79.67 (the manual calls it CPR; value shown with decimals, no % sign) and total memo 572, then brand-wise target, sales, % and memo count. Brands exceeding target show over 100 % (Black Diamond 415.17 %, Salmon 689.09 %); no capping.
- All numbers are in Bangla digits with Latin-style comma grouping and dot decimals; heading text Zone/Route names and brand names are Latin.

## Colours and style
- Top bar deep purple (indigo-violet) with white text and back arrow. Page lavender-white.
- Zone card: grey-teal / sage tint (distinct from the white route cards). Route cards: white, rounded (about 24 px), soft shadow.
- Table header text blue-purple (deep purple), body text dark grey/black. Hairline grey separators.
- "বিস্তারিত →": black bold text with black arrow, right aligned.

- No colour bands (red/amber/green) on percent values; all percent figures are plain dark text. No packshot images.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Per the manual: tap "বিস্তারিত" on a route (or the zone) card to open the brand detail for that route (RIGHT screen, title is the route name).
- Back arrow returns to the summary. Cards scroll vertically; brand table scrolls vertically.
- The screen is opened from the Reports menu tile "এখন পর্যন্ত বিক্রয় সারসংক্ষেপ" (screenshot 71, left).
