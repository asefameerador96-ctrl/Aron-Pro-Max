Two phone screenshots side by side, both full white pages with an illustration. Left: a prompt that updates were found with an আপডেট button. Right: the app-updating (data update) progress screen.

## Data and figures shown
- Progress step count "১৪/৮৭" = 14 of 87 (Bangla digits); the bar is filled to about 14/87 (about 16 percent).
- Version "1.0.1" for the current SR App (this differs from 1.0.25 on the login and download screens).
- The 87 is probably the number of data tables or files being updated (the update here is for synced data, not the APK).

## Colours and style
- White pages (not the light grey of the other app screens), so these look like separate, older-style screens.
- Left button: red-to-purple vertical gradient with white text and icon.
- Right: indigo heading, blue progress fill, blue count text, red tip text.
- Text on both screens is in a serif-style Bangla and Latin face.
- Illustrations are flat vector, blue/pink/purple.

## Navigation and behaviour
- The left screen appears inside the app when the server says required data/app updates exist; the manual says to tap আপডেট and wait.
- The right screen shows the update running: a step counter "প্রসেসিং(n/total)" and a bar; the user must not close the app.
- It blocks use of the app until finished ("you must update").
- No back button or skip is visible on either screen.

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual abbreviates the prompt as "Some new updates have been found..." in English; the image shows Bangla only: কিছু নতুন আপডেট পাওয়া গেছে. এই অ্যাপটি ব্যবহার চালিয়ে যেতে, আপনাকে অবশ্যই আপডেট করতে হবে!
- The image shows the footer "Current version SR App 1.0.1"; the manual does not mention it.
- The image shows the progress as "প্রসেসিং(১৪/৮৭)" with Bangla digits; the manual says only "with progress".
- The tip is the Bangla "টিপস: অ্যাপটি বন্ধ করবেন না", matching the manual's "do not close the app".
- The version 1.0.1 here conflicts with 1.0.25 in screenshots 03, 06 and 07.
