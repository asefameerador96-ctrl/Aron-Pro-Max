## Navigation and behaviour
- Manual: after the photo step, Collect DRP Discount opens this slide screen with SKUs, "খালি প্যাকেট" (empty packs) and the OFFER tag; tapping the offer shows the offer pop-up with its dates. The SR taps Collect DRP Discount and checks the offer.
- In the image: the ribbon opens the "ছাড়" pop-up (inferred; the manual says "the offer pop-up"). The yellow বন্ধ করুন closes it. The back chevron returns. Stepper plus/minus change the count; tapping the count opens the quick-entry pop-up (screenshot 34).
- জমা দিন at the bottom saves the collected empty packs.

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual calls the pop-up "the offer pop-up"; the image titles it "ছাড়" (Discount), in English the text is a DRP empty-pack offer, not a price discount.
- The manual says the SR taps "Collect DRP Discount"; that button is not visible in this image (it is on an earlier screen).
- Offer example text and dates match the manual (100 stick ... MaxR 10s, with dates).
