## Navigation and behaviour
- Tap a pack tile (screenshot 25 / right panel here) to open this entry page for that SKU.
- Fill quantities per fault type, then সংরক্ষণ saves the line and returns to the QC page, where it appears in QC সারাংশ.
- বাতিল leaves without saving; ডিলিট removes an existing QC line for the SKU (inferred from the label; not in the manual).
- QC জমা দিন submits the whole QC.
- The page scrolls (top card cut off).

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual says "quantity in sticks"; the image shows quantities in the summary and entry only as bare numbers (no unit) and a money cap in taka (৫৯৪.৫০৳), with "সর্বোচ্চ QC / QC হয়ে গেছে / বাকি আছে" money lines the manual does not mention.
- The manual says "total QC and settlement amount"; the image shows "মোট QC নিষ্পত্তি" (a count) and "সেটেলমেন্ট পরিমান" (an amount).
- Buttons বাতিল and ডিলিট are not mentioned in the manual.
- The manual lists only two fault types; the image shows six specific fault reasons under them (three each).

- Mixed digit styles: Latin "5" typed, Bangla digits in totals.
