## Data and figures shown
- Test data only. The alphabet bar lacks C, D, E, P, Q, T, U, W, X, Z, so it appears to show only initials that exist.
- The fields are pre-filled from the chosen outlet.
- The Save button is below the crop (not visible); the manual says "save".

## Colours and style
- Header gradient red (about #D94A4A) to purple (about #4A1A9A).
- Alphabet bar: dark grey #505050, white letters, selected chip with red-to-purple gradient.
- Inputs: white with purple outline; cards white on light grey page.
- GEO button: purple (left) to crimson (right) gradient.
- Yellow dot about #F5B921.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Manual: outlet drop-down and alphabet bar, cluster, shop name, owner, mobile, GEO and photo, save. The SR corrects the information, captures the photo at the shop, saves and confirms. The AMO verifies.
- Tapping a letter filters the outlet list; "All" shows all.
- Field order: outlet, (alphabet filter), cluster, shop name, owner, mobile, GEO+photo, Save.
