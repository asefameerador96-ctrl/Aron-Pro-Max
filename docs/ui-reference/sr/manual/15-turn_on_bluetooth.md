# 15. Turn on Bluetooth

## Screens in the image
Two phone screens side by side, both the Android system Bluetooth settings page (a Samsung One UI style, dark theme, handwriting-style font). Not part of the ARON app. Left: Bluetooth Off. Right: Bluetooth On with a nearby printer found under "Available devices".

## Layout, top to bottom
Left screen:
1. Black background. Top bar: back chevron at the left, title "Bluetooth" in a script-like font, three-dot menu at the right.
2. A dark grey rounded bar: "Off" at the left, a grey toggle switch (knob left) at the right.
3. Grey hint text under the bar.
4. Empty black page below.
Right screen:
1. Top bar: back chevron, "Bluetooth", "Stop" (text action) and the three-dot menu.
2. Dark grey rounded bar: "On" in blue at the left; a small blue-and-green scanning dots icon and a blue toggle (knob right) at the right.
3. Grey explanatory paragraph.
4. Section label "Available devices" (grey), then one dark rounded row: a printer icon and the name "RPP02N".
5. Empty black page below; a thin home-indicator bar at the bottom of both screens.

## Data and figures shown
- The printer's name is "RPP02N" (the RPP02N-class 58 mm mobile printer).
- The phone's name "MD's A12" (a Samsung Galaxy A12) is visible in the text.
- No other devices are listed yet (only the printer) at the moment of the capture.

## Colours and style
- System dark theme: black background, dark grey (#1C1C1E-ish) rounded bars, white text, blue accent (On and the toggle), grey secondary text.
- Samsung handwriting-style system font (a phone setting, not the app's font).
- No ARON gradient, no ARON icons; this is a stock Android screen.

## Navigation and behaviour
- The SR switches the printer on, switches the phone's Bluetooth on here (from the Android settings or the quick-settings panel), then taps "RPP02N" in the list to start pairing (next screenshot).
- "Stop" ends the scan; the list refreshes while scanning.
- Back returns to the Android Settings list (and then to the app).

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual says "Phone Settings > Bluetooth off, then on; the printer (e.g. RPP02N) under Available devices": matches the two screens exactly.
- The manual's "tap the printer name" is the row "RPP02N".
- The image is from a different phone language and theme than the field phones may have (English, dark); labels on a Bangla-language phone will differ.
