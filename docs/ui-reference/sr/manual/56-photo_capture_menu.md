## Navigation and behaviour
- Manual: "Two tiles: আস্থা (Astha) and Campaigns. Astha photo capture checks the geo-fence first ('জিওফেন্সিং পরীক্ষা করা হচ্ছে')." What to do: "Choose Astha or Campaigns, then the outlet."
- Tapping আস্থা opens the photo capture page; choosing the outlet starts the geo-fence check (spinner). After a pass the gift photo screen follows (screenshot 57, not in this range).
- Campaigns tile: not described beyond the label in the manual.

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual says the check happens first; the image shows the spinner with the Submit button already red (enabled-looking) while the check runs.
- The title "Photo Capture" is English; the Astha page title is Bangla.
- The manual does not explain the amount "৭৭৭.৫০ ৳" beside the retailer name, nor the blue dot.
