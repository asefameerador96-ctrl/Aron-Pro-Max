# 63. Settings and language

## Screens in the image
One phone screen: the "সেটিং" (Settings) page with the language pop-up menu open over the first tile. The image is cropped at the top (no status bar) and at the bottom.

## Layout, top to bottom
1. Top bar, red-to-purple gradient (about #D94A45 to #4F1D9A), white back chevron at left, centred white bold Bangla title "সেটিং".
2. Page background light grey (about #E8E8E8).
3. A row of three white-grey rounded tiles (about #F4F4F4, thin border, small shadow below):
   - Tile 1 (left): mostly hidden by the pop-up; only its edges show. It is the language tile.
   - Tile 2 (middle): a round icon (red-pink at top to blue at bottom) with a globe and arrows to a monitor, label "PDA টু সাপোর্ট" in dark purple.
   - Tile 3 (right): a round purple gradient icon with a white "sign out" arrow, label "লগ আউট" in dark purple.
4. Over tile 1: a white-lilac pop-up menu (about #FCF5FF, shadow) with two items, each with a black flag icon: "en" and "বাংলা", text in purple-grey.
5. Rest of the page is empty.

## Data and figures shown
- No figures. The language drop-down offers two choices: en and বাংলা.

## Colours and style
- Gradient top bar; light grey page; tiles pale grey with 12 dp radius.
- Icons: gradient circles (pink-red to blue for PDA, purple for logout); labels dark purple (#3B1A7A), Bangla font.
- Pop-up: very light lilac, small radius, soft shadow; flag icons black; item text purple-grey (#6A4C93).

## Navigation and behaviour
- Manual (section 24, screenshot 63): the gear icon on the Dashboard opens সেটিং (Settings) with language (en / বাংলা), PDA টু সাপোর্ট (send data file to support) and লগ আউট (Log Out). The SR chooses en for English or বাংলা for Bangla.
- Tapping a flag item switches the app language; whether this applies at once or needs a restart is not shown here (screenshot 64 shows a configuration dialog that closes the app, but that is for a different tile).

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual lists three items; the image (screenshot 64) shows a fourth tile, a green gear/download icon labelled "Upd..." (cut off), on a second row. It is not visible in this crop.
- The manual says the language tile has "en / বাংলা"; here the pop-up shows the flag icon and the language name for each.
- The Settings tile itself (on the Dashboard) is not shown.
