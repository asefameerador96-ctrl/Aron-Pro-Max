## Navigation and behaviour
- Manual: "Left: Astha outlets with name, owner, phone, Sub-Channel (Gold, Diamond...), Wing to Zone; search box. Right: one outlet's brand target table." What to do: "Search or scroll, tap an outlet, choose year, quarter and month(s)."
- Search filters by name, code or phone (placeholder). Tapping an outlet card leads to its brand target page (right screen), where year, quarter, month are chosen.

## Differences between the image and the manual text

- The right screen shows no outlet name or selected month; the manual says the table is for one outlet.
