## Data and figures shown
- Day cards: outlets visited / route outlets (39/70, so 39 visited of 70); strike rate 55% (39 of 70 is 55.7%, so it matches; the manual's example is the same); stock issued 12,148; current stock 10,621; not visited 31 (70 - 39 = 31, consistent); no-sale (zero memo) 0.
- Sales card: cigarette 9,890.00 + lighter 325.00 + match 28.00 = 10,243.00 (adds up); less discount 1,234.50 less QC 0.00 = 9,008.50 (adds up). Two decimals on money.
- The big "১,৫০০" under "বিক্রয় (স্টিক)" does not equal any of the other figures; it looks like a count in sticks (or memos) rather than taka. Uncertain what it counts.
- Second card repeats the discount (1,234.50) and QC (0.00) and adds DRP discount 0.00, all total 10,243.00, total taka 9,008.50.
- Left screen: STD achievement bar full green with a figure such as 1235% (test account); the cut-off cards read 0/61, 0%, 0, 0 (test SR with no visits).
- Dates are Latin digits, yyyy-mm-dd; all other figures are Bangla digits with Indian-style grouping not used (12,148 is western grouping).

## Colours and style
- Header and the Sales and Target cards: red-to-purple gradient (crimson #D0404B-ish to deep purple #4F1F99-ish); white text. The second card is a muted plum-to-mauve variant.
- Page: light grey; tiles white, thin light-grey border, about 12 dp radius, icons in gradient pink/magenta-purple-blue (the outlined Sales Journey icon, orange/brown Astha logo and photo-style Photo Capture icon are exceptions).
- Day cards: white with a purple (about #5B2A9E) 1.5 dp outline, rounded; figure large dark, label small.
- Progress bar: solid green with white percentage text.
- English labels (Photo Capture, Match Program) are set in purple and look larger than the Bangla labels.
- No packshots on this screen.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Tap a tile to open that feature (order and names as in the manual). Gear icon opens Settings.
- "বিস্তারিত" opens the Target and Achievement by SKU screen (screenshot 10).
- The page scrolls; the header collapses to a shorter height when scrolled (right screen).
- Day cards and the sales cards are read-only summaries that update during the day.

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- Tile list matches the manual (15 tiles, same order). Manual says "gear icon (Settings)" at the top: matches.
- The manual says "Top: SR name and ID, route and date": in the image the route and "Daily" run together ("Apsis RouteDaily", "Agora GoliDaily"); the route name carries no visit-day list here (the Attendance screen does).
- The manual lists the sales card as "Cigarette, Lighter, Match, total, discount, QC and grand total": matches. The image also shows a big "বিক্রয় (স্টিক) ১,৫০০" block at the left that the manual does not mention.
- The manual's "below: total discount, DRP discount, total QC, grand total and total taka": matches, but the manual does not say that the DRP discount is shown (value 0.00).
- The manual calls the progress bar "STD progress bar"; the image labels it "এস টি ডি".
- The manual's example day cards (39/70, 55%, 12,148, 10,621, 31, 0) match the right screen exactly. The left screen is a different (test) account.
- Day cards: the image has six cards in two rows (four plus two), not as one list.
