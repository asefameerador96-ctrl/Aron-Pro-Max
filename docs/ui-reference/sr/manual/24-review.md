## Data and figures shown
- Items: Aster lighter, quantity 25 (one box of 25), value 312.50 (12.50 each).
- Discount: two lines of Aster with quantity 0, values 2.50 and 10.00, total 12.50.
- Grand total: quantity 25, value 300.00 = 312.50 - 12.50, so the discount values are subtracted.
- The checkbox is unticked (cash sale).
- The outlet here is Hafiz Store, code 2650259, phone 123 (placeholder), cluster Agora Road; the same route as Ruku Store in screenshots 19 to 22.
- Digits are Bangla throughout this page.
- The discount lines contradict the sponsor rule that the offer-discount line stays zero in the rebuild (deferred programmes, docs/27); here the current app applies a discount of 12.50 on a lighter sale.

## Navigation and behaviour
- This page is shown after এগিয়ে যান from the quantity page. The SR checks quantities and discount.
- Ticking the checkbox marks the sale as due (credit).
- প্রোডাক্ট QC opens the product quality-check step (the manual covers it in the next section).
- প্রিন্ট submits the sale and prints the memo on the connected printer (per the manual: "Tap প্রিন্ট to submit").
- Back arrow returns to the Sales page to change quantities.

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual lists "মোট (total) and সর্বমোট (grand total)". The image shows these in three places: "মোট লাইটার" (a lighter category total in the items table), "মোট" (the discount total) and "সর্বমোট" (the grand total).
- The manual says "ডিসকাউন্ট (discount / offers)"; the image shows two Aster discount lines with quantity 0 and a taka value, which implies the discount is a value, not a quantity.
- The image shows a green printer icon at the top right, not mentioned in the manual.
- Item rows show the SKU name only, with no packshots on this page (the manual does not say either way).
- The items table shows a category subtotal row "মোট লাইটার" in addition to the SKU row.
- The two screens are the same page one minute apart, not two different states (the manual does not say which is which).
