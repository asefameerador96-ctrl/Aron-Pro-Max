## Colours and style
- Dialog: lilac with big radius; title dark, bold, centred. Reason list: white-pink panel with a grey highlight on the first option. Cancel: amber gradient; Submit: red-to-purple gradient. - Background overlay about 50 percent black.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Manual: tapping এডিট asks for the reason (the list: ভুল SKU নির্বাচিত, ভুল SKU পরিমাণ, প্রমোশন ইস্যু); the SR chooses a reason and taps জমা দিন; then the Sales screen opens to change quantities; the SR corrects the sale and prints again. Editing is possible only inside the outlet's geo-fence and not after QC has been entered for the outlet.
- বাতিল closes the dialog without editing (inferred).
- The red crossed printer at the top-right in the right screen suggests printing is disabled for that state (reason not stated).

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual says "ভুল SKU নির্বাচিত" without the final danda; the image shows "ভুল SKU নির্বাচিত।" with the danda.
- The manual only mentions জমা দিন; the image also has a বাতিল (Cancel) button.
- The geo-fence and QC rules in the manual note are not visible as any message here.
- The "প্রমোশন ইস্যু" reason refers to promotions, which are deferred.
