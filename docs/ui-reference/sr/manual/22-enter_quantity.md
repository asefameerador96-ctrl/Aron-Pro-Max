## Data and figures shown
- Avon-20S: entered 20; cart total 140.00 taka. At the same prices as screenshot 23 (BDDF-20s 20 sticks = 130.00, i.e. 6.50 per unit), a price of 7.00 each is implied by 20 x 7 = 140.
- Avon: previous day 20, ADS 3.33, SDLW 20.
- Aster (lighter): entered 25; previous day, ADS and SDLW all N/A. Aster 25 units price is 312.50 in screenshot 24 (12.50 each).
- Lighter pack badge shows 25 (the lighter box size of 25), matching the owner's units rule.
- Quick buttons: cigarette steps 20, 100, 200, 400, 500, 1000 (positive and negative); lighter steps 1, 5, 10, 20, 25, 50.

## Colours and style
- Pop-up: pale lavender, very large corner radius; close icon red outlined circle with x.
- Quick-button panel: vertical gradient deep purple at top to red-orange at bottom; buttons are white with black Bangla digits.
- Stats panel: yellow-to-orange gradient with white text for the labels and values.
- Save button: purple-to-red vertical gradient with white text.
- Minus and plus buttons: purple-to-pink rounded squares with white symbols (on the Stock page minus was red and plus purple).
- The pack picture and purple round badge sit at the left; the number field is Latin digits ("20", "25") while buttons and stats use Bangla digits.

## Navigation and behaviour
- Tapping an SKU card on the Sales page opens this pop-up. The minus and plus buttons change the quantity by 1 (assumed); each quick button adds or subtracts its amount.
- সংরক্ষণ saves the line and the cart total in the bottom bar updates; the red circled x closes without saving (assumed).
- Then এগিয়ে যান (Proceed) goes to the review page (screenshot 24).
- Lighters use smaller steps, as the manual says.
