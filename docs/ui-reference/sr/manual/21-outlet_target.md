## Data and figures shown
- Pop-up: Target STD 2240, Achievement 390, Remaining 1850 (2240 - 390 = 1850, consistent). Target Memo 18, Achievement 7, Remaining 11 (18 - 7 = 11, consistent).
- Page table: nine SKU rows (the table has nine SKU rows: Avon-20S, ARIS-A-20s, BDDF-10s, BDDF-20s, Aster, FB, EssP-20S 20HL, EssCM-20S 20HL, Max) and three total rows. Cigarette target 140 equals the sum of the cigarette rows 20+40+10+30+20+20 = 140 (Aster, FB and Max being lighter/match items, with target 2, 1, 1; lighter total 3 = Aster 2 + FB 1; match 1 = Max 1).
- Sales 920 matches the amount ": ৯২০.০০ ৳" beside the outlet name in screenshot 19, so that amount is probably the outlet's month-to-date sales value. (The cigarette sales column is 20+20+20+80 = 140, not 920, so the 920 total does not equal the sum of the visible rows; see differences.)
- The pop-up target (2240 STD) and the page target (140 cigarette) are on different units or bases (STD vs SKU units); not explained.
- Percent: 920 / 140 = 657.14% as shown.
- Route is "Agora GoliDaily", cluster "Agora Goli, Gulshan-2".

## Differences between the image and the manual text
- The manual says the right page shows "each SKU's target, sales and %, and totals for Cigarette, Lighter and Match"; the image matches, but the cigarette sales total (920) does not equal the sum of the cigarette rows visible (140). The visible SKU sales cells may be quantities in packets while the total is in a different unit or value (taka); needs checking.
- The manual lists the pop-up fields; the image shows them with the English labels exactly as in the manual, and the numbers in Latin digits (2240, 390, ...), while the page table uses Bangla digits.
- Pop-up fields are in English and the page headings in Bangla; the manual does not mention this mix.
- The manual calls the campaign "Super Star Low, November"; the image shows "Super Star Low (November)".
- The manual does not mention that the route name appears as "Agora GoliDaily" (the word Daily joined without a space).
- Targets and loyalty tiers are deferred in the rebuild (docs/27).
