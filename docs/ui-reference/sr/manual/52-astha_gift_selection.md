## Navigation and behaviour
- Manual: "Choose the retailer; outlet code, route, category (Gold) and cluster; 'একটি গিফট নির্বাচন করুন' (choose a gift), e.g. Hotpot 3 bati, Feature Phone; সাবমিট." What to do: "Choose the gift the outlet wants and submit."
- Choosing a letter filters the retailer list; choosing a retailer fills the detail card and gift cards. Submit is disabled until a gift is chosen (inferred from the grey look only; the image shows no selected card).
- Reached from the Astha menu tile গিফট নির্বাচন (screenshot 48).
