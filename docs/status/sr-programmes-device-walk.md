# SR programme screens: a walk on a real phone

For the owner. About 40 minutes with one SR test phone. Plain steps: what to tap, what you should see, what to photograph. If something looks different from what is written here, photograph it and write the step number next to it; that is the useful result.

Written 2026-11-25 for the SR app as built on the integration branch (programme screens: Target, KPI, Lifting Star, Outlet target, Astha, Gift choice, Loyalty points, Redemption, Photos).

## Before you start

1. Use an SR test login on a phone that has a data connection, and open the app once on Wi-Fi so it can download today's route and figures.
2. Check in for the day (Attendance), then pull the sync once (tap the small sync chip at the top). Wait until it says everything is sent.
3. Pick the test route that has Gulshan-Banani outlets, if you are asked to choose a route.
4. Keep the phone in Bangla for steps 1 to 11 (the normal way the reps use it). Step 12 repeats a few screens in English.

Every figure from the server shows a line such as "Server figures through <date>". That date is the last full day the server counted. Anything the phone adds from today's selling is always written on its own separate line that starts "Today on this phone". The two are never added together. That separation is the main thing to look at all through this walk.

## The walk

**1. Target and achievement by SKU (Home)**
Tap: on Home, find the card titled "Today's progress" or "Target & Achievement (SKU List)" and tap Details.
You should see: a list of products, each with its target, what has been sold, what is left and a percentage. Cigarettes are counted in packets, matches in dozens, lighters in boxes. A product the server has no figure for shows a dash, never a zero.
Photograph: the top of the list, and one product row up close.

**2. KPI**
Tap: the KPI tile on Home.
You should see: the month card at the top (days done out of working days, the three big percentages), then the category bars. Tap "See all brands".
Brand wise KPI: a list of brands. A brand that has both cigarettes and matches shows each in its own unit, written side by side (for example "100 packet · 30 dozen"), with a dash for its percentage. A brand where the phone is not sure of the unit shows a dash and a short note.
Photograph: the month card, and the brand list.

**3. Match Box Lifting Star**
Tap: the Match Program tile on Home.
You should see: a card with Target, STD, Projection, Achievement, last month's figures, Status and the slab pills, plus a line about last month if it is not closed yet. If the server has told the phone which products count for this programme, a line near the bottom says "Today on this phone: ..." with what this phone has sold since the last full day. If the phone cannot be sure, that line says "may be incomplete" or shows dashes, and says why in a short sentence below it.
Do this: make one small sale of a Star product to any outlet, come back to this screen, and check that only the "Today on this phone" line moved, and the STD above it did not.
Photograph: the whole card before the sale, and the bottom lines after it.

**4. Outlet target pop-up and page (during a call)**
Tap: Sale, choose an outlet, start the call. When the app asks about today's target, tap it.
You should see: a small box with the outlet name, the campaign name, "Server figures through <date>", target / achieved / remaining for the STD and for the memos, and an Okay button. Tap "View SKU targets" for the full page: each product with its target, sold and percent, and a total per category.
If the download was cut for this outlet you will see a purple note saying so, and the category totals show dashes instead of numbers. That is correct. A cut outlet still shows the server's own achievement and remaining numbers.
Photograph: the pop-up, the page, and (if you can find one) an outlet with the purple "cut" note.

**5. Astha target**
Tap: the Astha tile, then Target.
You should see: month buttons across the top, and tabs for the STD table by brand and for the Memo target. Tap each month button and each tab.
Photograph: the STD tab and the Memo tab.

**6. Gift selection**
Tap: Astha, then Gift selection. Pick an outlet and choose one gift.
You should see: only the gifts that outlet's category may have, and after you tap Submit the line "Chosen on this phone, not sent yet". After the next sync it should change to "Sent to the server". Once the hand-over photo is taken the choice is locked and says so.
Photograph: the list of gifts, and the line after the sync.

**7. Loyalty points**
Tap: the Loyalty Point tile, then Points.
You should see: the outlet's points and what is not confirmed yet, if anything.
Photograph: the points screen.

**8. Redemption**
Tap: the Loyalty Point tile, then Redemption.
You should see: the gifts the outlet can redeem for, and, after you submit one, a clear line saying it is waiting for the server. Nothing should say "done" until the server has said yes.
Photograph: the screen before and after Submit.

**9. Gift hand-over photo and campaign photo**
Tap: the Photo Capture tile, then Astha (gift hand-over) and then Campaign.
You should see: for Astha, the gift you chose in step 6 (if no gift is held it tells you to choose one first); a camera; a "Checking geofencing" moment; then "Photo saved on this phone". It uploads on Wi-Fi first.
Photograph: each of the three states (before the photo, saving, saved).

**10. Sales Journey**
Tap: the Sales Journey tile, if it opens a screen on your build.
You should see: the day's calls in order with their results.
Photograph: the list.

**11. Work with no data connection**
Do this: switch on aeroplane mode. Open steps 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 again.
You should see: the same figures as before, and a line saying you are offline and seeing the last download. Nothing should crash, spin forever or show zeros that were not there before.
Photograph: step 3 and step 4 while offline.

**12. English and big text**
Do this: switch the language to English, and (Settings, Display) make the text as large as it goes. Open steps 1, 3 and 4 again.
You should see: the same words in English, nothing cut off at the edge, and nothing hidden behind a button. Switch back to Bangla afterwards.
Photograph: step 3 and step 4 in English, and one screen at the large text size.

## Things that must never happen

- A server figure that changes because you sold something. Only "Today on this phone" lines may change before the next sync.
- A zero where the screen cannot know the number. Unknown is a dash, or the words "at least".
- Two different units added into one number (sticks and dozens and boxes together).
- A figure with no date beside it.

## What to send back

The photographs, with the step number in each file name, plus one line per step: "as written", or what was different. The server team uses the differences to decide the next round.
