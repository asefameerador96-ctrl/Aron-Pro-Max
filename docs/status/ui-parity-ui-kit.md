## SCALE OF THE REFERENCE SCREENSHOTS (conclusion, 2026-10-19; lead decision: keep the PARITY-SPEC dp sizes)
The owner's screenshots were taken on a device that draws far more dp across than a field phone, so their proportions cannot be copied 1:1 onto a 360 to 411 dp phone. Evidence: on sr/attendance.png (1258 px wide) the Roboto body line "RC38+J3Q, ..." has a capital height of 17 px and a line pitch of 36 px; Roboto's capital is 0.71 em, so at the usual 14 to 16 sp that is about 1.5 to 1.9 px per dp, i.e. a screen about 650 to 850 dp wide (a tablet, or a phone at its smallest display size). Read at that width, the reference's minus button (21 units of a 411-unit-wide image) is about 36 dp and its top bar (36 units) about 56 to 60 dp: exactly the PARITY-SPEC values (36 dp stepper, standard 56 dp bar). So the original app on a 360 to 411 dp field phone draws what the kit draws: the kit keeps the spec dp sizes (48 dp touch targets, readable Bangla) and matches the reference in colour, weight, radius, layout and proportion within them. Side-by-side evidence (docs/evidence/ui-parity/kit-*.png) therefore shows our elements larger than the reference at the same image width; that is the device, not a defect. Where the Vision QA asked for ratio-based sizes (22 dp stepper, 36 to 40 dp bar, 12 sp tile labels, tiny table headers) the kit keeps the spec values.
