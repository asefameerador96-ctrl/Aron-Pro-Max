## Found and fixed: the Gulshan–Banani data-flow trace (docs/34 s4.4)
qa traced one SR selling day end to end: seed → the phone's model → sync → tables → warehouse → national dashboard → reports, 15 hops, 155 checks, each number next to the device's own figure (`docs/evidence/data-flow-trace-2026-10-23.md`). Money and quantities were identical at every hop (gross 2,487,000 milli-taka, paid 1,908,600, due 578,400; sold 180 cigarette sticks, 75 bidi sticks, 50 lighters, 5 match dozens; one voided memo excluded), idempotency held under duplicate, replay, reorder, tampered payload and a late sale, and scope held for Wing Manager, DMO and TSO. It found seven differences; all seven are closed on dev:
1. **Pack size was not re-checked on the server.** Two packets of a 20-stick SKU sent with a pack factor of 10 were accepted and booked as 20 sticks and the money of 20 sticks instead of 40. Now such a line, its memo and the memo's other records are parked `qty_invalid`, retryable after a bundle refresh, and nothing of the memo is booked (D-BC-PACK-01).
2. **Memo numbers were never validated.** Another user's number, another date or an out-of-block number was accepted, and a forged number could turn the real owner's memo into a duplicate. Now the sequence must sit in one of the user's four blocks or overflow ranges for that business date; a memo refused this way takes its children with it (D-BC-MEMONO-01).
3. **Report totals added sticks, pieces and dozens into one number** (by-outlet, route-STD and DS-RRS totals). By-outlet now carries one base-unit column per category, fed by V0109; the cross-category totals are gone.
4. A Brand user got 403 on every dashboard card (part trace-setup bug, part wiring); fixed in train 10, proven by the scope-leak test.
5. Web tokens carried no `geo` claim; added as information only, the server still resolves reach from the rows (D-AUTHZ-GEO-CLAIM-01).
6. A doc named a warehouse table that does not exist; corrected.
7. The Final Submit preview warned about a route not due that day; ruled: every active route is listed, warnings count only due routes (D-BC-PREVIEW-01). A `planned` flag for the "not due" greying is queued for contract 1.7.

Not proven by this trace (next slices, already assigned): the half-up and `round_adj` money branches (every SKU in the day had a per-base quantity of 1 and whole-taka prices), a multi-month target reading, concurrency and batch reuse at trace level (the sync fuzz suite covers concurrency separately).
