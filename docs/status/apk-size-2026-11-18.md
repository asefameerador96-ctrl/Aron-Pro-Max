## 5. Why AMO grew as much as SR without programmes screens

1. AMO has its own large feature module (`feature.amo` +210 KB dex, the largest single package) and the heaviest of the new art: 43 new resources, 75 KB of traced vector XML plus 23 KB of webp, against 8.7 KB for SR.
2. It shares every touched core and feature module (core +125 KB, sale, memo, map, database) and the Compose and WorkManager code they pull in.
3. It gained maps-compose (+54 KB) and the AMO map screen; AMO also loses less from the scanner change in relative terms because its base was smaller.
4. AMO's smaller baseline (5.0 MB) makes the same bytes a larger percentage.

## 6. R8 and keep rules

Compared R8 `configuration.txt` base vs INT: no keep rule was added since 6 Nov. The only difference is removal (ML Kit and `UsedBy` keeps). Nothing is being kept that should not be because of a new rule. Apps are minified and resource-shrunk; `localeFilters` is already en and bn.

## 7. Safe reductions (none applied)

| # | Change | Est. saving (arm64 download) | Risk |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Simplify or rasterise the AMO traced vector art (`amo_art_*`, `amo_update_*`): vectors of 4 to 7 KB each as webp of the same size are 1 to 3 KB; or ask design for simpler paths | 40 to 70 KB amo | low; visual check against parity spec |
| 2 | Drop `assets/PublicSuffixDatabase.list` from OkHttp (132 KB raw, 42 KB compressed) if no cookie/public-suffix matching is needed (API is one host) | about 40 KB each app | low; needs OkHttp behaviour check, test with R8 |
| 3 | Enable `-repackageclasses` / `-allowaccessmodification` and Intrinsics removal (`-assumenosideeffects kotlin.jvm.internal.Intrinsics`) | 60 to 120 KB each | low to medium; null-check messages vanish, run full test and golden pass |
| 4 | Convert large png/webp art to lossy webp q80 where lossless | 10 to 20 KB | low |
| 5 | Prune resources.arsc: drop unused string and style entries (lint `UnusedResources`, `resourceShrinker` strict mode); arsc doubled in sr (+94 KB) | 20 to 50 KB each | low |
| 6 | Check unused library surface: CameraX camera2 interop (+86 KB sr), Firebase pieces in AMO (+28 KB), maps-compose in apps that need the map only on one screen | 30 to 80 KB | medium; needs owner of each feature |
| 7 | Fonts: subset the two Bangla font families to the glyphs the app uses (8 ttf at about 5 x 75 KB + 2 x 17 KB compressed); biggest single non-code item, 5 of the files are 130 KB raw each | 100 to 200 KB each app | medium; Bangla shaping must be tested (conjuncts), print path uses its own bitmap path |
| 8 | Ship only arm64 + armv7 splits in Play (already the "download" view) | none needed | n/a |

Total realistic: about 200 to 350 KB per app without touching behaviour (items 1 to 5), up to about 500 KB with font subsetting and library pruning. That would bring sr to roughly +2 to +4% and amo to roughly +3 to +5% against the 6 Nov baseline, back under the warn line.

## 8. Recommendation

The growth is explained and legitimate; the absolute size (5.5 to 5.8 MB) is far inside the 30 MB budget. Cheapest first steps: items 2, 3 and 5 (no visual change), then item 1 with design sign-off. Font subsetting (7) gives the largest single win but needs a Bangla device walk. Re-baselining is the lead's call and is not done here.
