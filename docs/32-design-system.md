# 32 — Design direction: "Calm Glass" (binding, 2026-10-07; tokens v0 by the lead, v1 in docs/design/)

**Sponsor ask:** the UI must look fantastic and modern, in the spirit of Apple's interfaces: clean, spacious, glassy (glassmorphism), with smooth motion, in all four front ends (SR, AMO, TSO apps; web dashboard; admin portal). **Constraint that comes with it:** the SR app runs a full day on a shared 2 GB phone in sunlight on a 2 GB data pack, so the look must never cost speed, legibility or battery (docs/04, docs/31 s1).

## 1. Principles
1. **Clarity first.** One primary action per screen, large type for numbers, generous space, plain Bangla labels. A salesperson in the sun with one hand must read and tap without thinking. Legibility outranks glass (section 2a).
2. **Layered glass, restrained.** Soft translucent surfaces over a calm gradient background: hairline inner border, gentle shadow, large radius. Glass is a surface treatment, not decoration on every element.
3. **Depth with meaning.** Elevation shows hierarchy (page, card, sheet, dialog); a sheet that covers the page is the only place a strong blur is allowed.
4. **Motion that explains.** 150 to 250 ms spring or ease-out transitions, shared-element between list and detail, haptic tick on confirm; no decorative loops; respect the system "remove animations".
5. **Bangla-native.** Typography tuned for Bengali (taller line height, no tight letter spacing), Bengali digits per locale, labels never truncated mid-conjunct.
6. **Offline is visible and calm.** A small, steady status chip (Offline, Syncing, Synced, N waiting) on every main screen: never a blocking modal.

## 2. Glass tiers (performance contract)
| Tier | Where | Treatment | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A, full glass** | web (desktop browsers), phones of performance class high (Android 12 and later, 4 GB+, not in battery saver) when `cfg.app.ui_glass` allows | real backdrop blur on sheets, nav bars and cards, vibrancy tint, animated highlights | blur radius and number of blurred layers capped; measured on a reference high-end phone |
| **B, glass-lite (DEFAULT on field phones)** | Galaxy A06, A07, Honor X5c Plus and similar | tinted translucent surfaces, soft gradient sheen, hairline borders, soft shadows, **no runtime blur layers** | must hold the frame-time gate on the A06 (docs/31 s1) |
| **C, solid** | battery saver, "reduce transparency", sunlight high-contrast theme, any phone that fails the frame-time probe | opaque surfaces, same layout, same radii | text contrast at least WCAG AA 4.5:1, key numbers AAA |
The tier is chosen at runtime (device class, battery saver, accessibility settings) and can be forced by the admin config key `cfg.app.ui_glass` (`auto`, `lite`, `off`) and by a user setting. The same screens exist in all tiers; only the surface treatment changes.

## 2a. Field legibility rule: outdoor-first (owner decision 2026-10-07, binding, overrides any glass choice below)
**Owner decision:** the owner asked for the Apple-like glass look, and said that where glass could hurt a salesperson reading the screen in sunlight, the lead's recommendation wins, while the apps must still look fancy and meet a clear standard. Legibility is therefore a gate; glass is a finish.

1. **Content is solid, chrome is glass.** In the SR, AMO and TSO apps every surface that carries a number, a price, a quantity, an outlet name, a memo line, a status or a primary action is **opaque** (at least 96 percent opacity, never a translucent layer over a gradient or image). Glass is allowed only on chrome that carries no critical reading: top app bar background, bottom navigation, sheet handles and scrims, the status chip's halo, home tile backgrounds that have a solid label plate, and empty-state backdrops. The web dashboard and admin portal (office use) may use full tier A glass.
2. **Default theme for field phones is light**, high contrast (deep ink text on near-white solid cards over a calm cool gradient). Dark theme stays available as a user choice but is not the default (a dark screen reflects glare worse in the sun).
3. **Contrast targets (computed from tokens, not eyeballed):** key figures (money, quantity, memo number, outlet name, primary button label) at least 7:1 (WCAG AAA) in every tier; body text at least 7:1 in tier B for field apps and 4.5:1 minimum on any desk screen; secondary text at least 4.5:1, never light grey; disabled and placeholder text still 3:1. No information by colour alone (icon plus label for success, warning, error, offline).
4. **Glare proxy (testable):** blend both foreground and background colours 35 percent toward white (a screen under direct sun reflects light); key figures must still reach 4.5:1 and body text 3:1 after the blend. Colours that fail it (for example mid-blue text on white) are not allowed for text. **Exception (lead ruling 2026-10-07, from the kit lane's measurement):** a primary-action label set in large bold type (at least 18 sp bold, as every 56 dp primary button) on a filled accent needs 7:1 normally and only 3:1 after the glare blend, because a white label on a blue fill that keeps 7:1 cannot also keep 4.5:1 under a 35 percent white veil; every other text keeps the 4.5:1 and 3:1 limits.
5. **Sunlight mode** (tier C plus): a sun icon in the top bar of the SR app (one tap, remembered per user) and a one-time suggestion chip when the system screen-brightness setting is at 85 percent or more, read once when a screen resumes (no light sensor, no polling, no battery cost). It forces opaque surfaces, removes every gradient behind text, raises all text contrast to 7:1 and key figures to 10:1 where the palette allows, adds one weight step (Bangla body at least weight 500), raises body type one step (Latin 17 sp, Bangla 18/28 sp), thickens hairlines to 2 dp and focus rings to 3 dp, and keeps the same layout. It also engages with battery saver and "reduce transparency".
6. **Type and shape for legibility:** no weight below 400 for Latin and 500 (Medium) for Bangla in every theme and tier (body and caption), because Bengali conjuncts go thin on low-resolution LCDs in sunlight; tabular figures for all money; minimum text size 14 sp (captions only), body 16 sp; touch targets 48 dp, primary actions 56 dp, bottom-aligned; no text over photos.
7. **Still fancy, at no cost to reading:** layered solid cards with soft low-opacity shadows and a 1 dp hairline (depth without blur), large radii, a confident accent colour, large tabular numerals as the hero of each screen, gradient and sheen only on large areas without small text (header backdrop, tiles, empty states, button sheen under 8 percent luminance change), spring motion and shared-element transitions, haptic ticks on confirm, polished Bangla typography, friendly illustrations on empty states, and a calm animated status chip.
8. **Gates (CI plus device):** (a) a unit test in core-ui computes the contrast of every declared token pair (text roles by surface roles, per tier, light, dark and sunlight) with the targets of items 3 and 4 and fails below them; the same for the web CSS variables; (b) screenshot tests of home, sale entry, review and memo in tier B and sunlight mode; (c) device check **D-UI-01** (owner, A06 outdoors at midday at full brightness, 5 minutes): home, sale entry, review and memo are readable without shading the screen with a hand; if it fails the targets above are raised, never lowered, without the lead's written approval.
The tier table below stays; tier B on field phones means "glass-lite chrome, solid content" as defined here.

## 3. Tokens v0 (the design workflow refines them into docs/design/tokens.md v1; lanes use the names, not hard-coded values)
- **Colour roles** (light / dark): `bg.gradient` (a calm cool gradient / deep slate gradient), `surface.glass` (white 62 percent / white 12 percent), `surface.solid`, `border.hairline` (white 45 percent / white 14 percent), `text.primary`, `text.secondary`, `accent` (a confident blue), `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `offline`. Money and quantity numbers use `text.primary` at the largest weight.
- **Shape:** radius 12 (chips), 20 (cards), 28 (sheets), full for buttons; hairline 1 dp.
- **Elevation:** four levels with soft, low-opacity shadows; no heavy drop shadows.
- **Type** (Noto Sans Bengali plus the Latin face the kit bundles, already budgeted in docs/31): display 34/40, title 22/30, body 16/24 (Bangla 16/26), caption 13/18; tabular figures for money.
- **Spacing:** 4 dp grid; screen padding 16 dp; touch targets at least 48 dp, primary actions 56 dp, bottom-aligned for one-hand use.
- **Motion:** `fast` 150 ms, `base` 220 ms, `sheet` 320 ms; spring for sheets; reduced-motion variant is a plain fade.

## 4. Rules for the lanes
- Android: build every screen from the `core-ui` kit (N-023, lane android-core-ui). No screen defines its own colours, radii or type. The kit exposes the glass tier through one `GlassSurface` composable. A backdrop-blur library (for example Haze) may be used for tier A only, and only if it passes the A06 frame-time probe without being loaded on tier B and C devices; adding a dependency needs a request file.
- Web: Tailwind with the same token names as CSS variables; `backdrop-filter` only on tier A, with a `@supports not` and a `prefers-reduced-transparency` fallback to solid; charts follow the `dataviz` skill (colour-blind safe, light and dark).
- Every screen ships its empty, loading, offline, error and long-text (Bangla) states, previews for the three tiers and for light and dark, and a screenshot or semantics test that runs in CI.
- Accessibility: contrast gates above, 200 percent font scale without clipping, TalkBack labels, no information by colour alone.
- The owner reviews the look on the real phones; the lead posts the screens in `docs/status/device-checks.md`.

## 5. Process
A design workflow produces `docs/design/` (tokens v1, Android glass spec, web glass spec, screen specs and an HTML preview of the key screens in light, dark, and the three tiers). The owner reviews the preview and says what to change; the kit and the web theme follow the approved version. Until v1 is approved the kit uses these v0 token names.
