## open-work-midnight verdict=fail
SUMMARY: Fail. Read from code only at 2ca39b11 (delta 04f8bf1a..2ca39b11), nothing run. Several parts work: the guard against an automatic close while the call is in memory, the visit-dated sale, QC, close and collection inside a live straddling call, the earliest-time rule limited to the check-in's day, and the B5 hold until a submit is acked. Five things are still wrong. (1) B1 fails after a kill: restoreOpenVisit and callToRestore read only today's visits, and the AMO does not restore at start at all. A call opened on D is lost on a relaunch after midnight, and D is closed automatically under it. The test bypasses the restore. (2) B2 is unreachable in practice: the 00:00 timer, Start, ON_RESUME and the call-ended effect all close D with the phone's own check-out before the rep can press his, so the after-midnight check-out never takes a location reading. A lead ruling is needed. (3) N1 is incomplete: AMO joint-call, assessment, distribution, price and survey records, and SR survey and content-view records, still take the clock's date. After midnight they are refused, and the SR survey is dropped silently. (4) 'Day gate null' opens every operational tile, not only the call. Stock, skip and tasks are dated D+1 and fail with a generic message or silence, and nothing tells the rep to finish the call. (5) Regression from the new collection fallback: any unclosed visit from yesterday, such as a blocked visit, locks a legitimate D+1 Mark Paid. No bypass of the stock gate was found: a straddling call sells against D's stock, and D+1 stock needs a D+1 check-in. An open call can, though, delay D's submit without limit at one outlet. No test was loosened.

### BLOCKING upheld=3/3: B1 not fixed after a kill and relaunch: an open call from day D is not restored after midnight, so D is closed automatically under it

## checkout-hold-refused verdict=fail
SUMMARY: FAIL on the B5 lens. Under the new nextSendable rule the check-out is held until a day_submit of its date is acked. Two cases work: Send again of an exhausted submit, and the release of an integrity-quarantined submit, because both keep their low seq. A day with no submit row releases the check-out at once. The way forward that the ruling and the text name, a cycle-2 submit, deadlocks: the cycle-2 row (higher seq) waits for the earlier pending check-out, and the check-out waits for an acked submit. I reproduced this in sqlite with the exact SQL. The test hides it by inserting an already-acked cycle-2 row. Second, the held check-out stays 'pending', and the manual Sales Submit gate counts pending rows over the whole outbox. So a submit refused for a cause no resend can cure (for example out of window after 7 days offline) disables manual Sales Submit on every later day. After midnight the screen can only submit today anyway. The Bangla and English texts tell the rep to 'tap Submit again or Send again', and neither is available for a server-refused submit, so the rep is trapped and the check-out never reaches the server. These are fixed: ruling 7, N3, the replay and skip lows, the CheckOutOrderSyncTest assertion and the DROPPED text. No test was loosened, DECISIONS.md is append-only and Room is still version 10. N4 is fixed as the lead worded it, but rank is only a proxy for parentage (see lows). Nothing was run with Gradle or on a device.

### BLOCKING upheld=3/3: B5 hold deadlocks a cycle-2 submit against the held check-out, so a refused submit can never be cured and the check-out never reaches the server
WHERE: android/core-database/src/main/kotlin/com/aktcl/aron/core/database/dao/OutboxDao.kt:44-48 (nextSendable); fixture shortcut in android/core-database/src/test/kotlin/com/aktcl/aron/core/database/CheckOutSubmitTest.kt:210
WHY: The day_submit hold (line 44) waits while ANY earlier row of its date is pending or in flight. The new attendance_event hold (lines 46-48) keeps the check-out pending until some day_submit of the date is acked. A cycle-2 submit is written after the check-out, so its seq is higher. The check-out is then an earlier pending row of the same date, so the cycle-2 submit is held by the check-out, and the check-out is held until a submit is acked. Neither can ever be sent. Before this delta the check-out was released once the submit was final, so there was no cycle. Ruling 5 and the lane's own answer (3a) name 'a cycle-2 submit' as the way forward, and that path is now dead. The test never exercises it: it INSERTS a cycle-2 row already in state 'acked' (line 210) instead of queuing it pending through recordDaySubmit, so the deadlock cannot show up. Only Send again of a retry_exhausted submit and the release of an integrity-quarantined one still work, because both keep the old low seq.
SCENARIO: SR checks out at 17:10 on D. The auto submit (seq 40) is followed by the check-out (seq 41). The server rejects the submit with a final code (for example business_date_out_of_window after 7 days offline, or schema_invalid), or a reviewer resolves a quarantined submit to rejected. The rep or a later build queues cycle 2 (seq 42, pending). nextSendable returns neither 41 (no acked submit on D) nor 42 (41 is an earlier pending row of D). I reproduced this with the exact WHERE clause in sqlite: rows [attendance acked, memo acked, day_submit rejected, attendance pending, day_submit pending] give nothing sendable for D. D's check-out and D's resubmit stay on the phone for ever, and the server never gets a check-out for D.
