# Lead review: android-gates slices 2 and 3 (check-in gate, check-out submits the sales)

Reviewed 2026-11-17 by the lead (Opus pass, three lenses, every blocking finding checked by three refuters). Range 40731485b..74f588332 on `lane/android-gates`. Verdict: FAIL, six blocking findings (below, raw), rulings in DECISIONS D-LEAD-PREREQ-03. Read from code only: nothing was built, run or walked on a device.

## checkin-gate verdict=fail
SUMMARY: Slice 2 puts the check-in gate in the right place: one pure function reads stored attendance inside every operational CaptureRepository transaction, it is switched on only in SrDay and AmoDay, and every SR and AMO operational writer I could find is covered. The exemptions match the rulings. The stale-midnight restore is fixed on SR (boundary and resume) and on AMO (resume only). The UI mirror shows CheckInFirst with a way to check in and to close an open call. One blocking finding: a call that straddles Dhaka midnight. The memo takes the date of 'now' (D+1), so the gate and the stock check refuse it. Slice 3's forgotten-day close also fires at the live 00:00 boundary and ignores the open call, so D is auto-submitted and auto-checked-out under an open call. The rep can either drop the sale or make a bogus 00:05 check-in for D+1, which takes up tomorrow's attendance and still fails the zero-stock check. Lower findings: check-out is decided by capturedAt order, so a stored check-out can be ignored after clock tampering; AMO has no boundary refresh and draws no tile lock; some read-only screens are closed; the 'each exemption proven' claim covers only some of them. Nothing was run on a device or in a build.

## checkout-submit verdict=fail
SUMMARY: Verdict: fail, with four blocking issues. Slice 3 (b7caeb46e..74f588332) gets the core right. The check-out and the day's submit(s) are one Room transaction, offline, all-or-nothing on a kill. Each route-day with captures gets its own submit. A manual Sales Submit that stands is not doubled in the normal case. The outbox holds a day's submit behind every earlier record of that date, and the check-out behind the submit (OutboxDao.nextSendable). The 17:00 boundary reads cfg.day.checkout_earliest_time. pending_count is the real number. No test was loosened, and the new strings are in Bangla and English resources.

The four blocking issues:
1. Once a manual Sales Submit stands and the zone's Final Submit has locked the route-day (late-sale policy set to quarantine or reject), AutoDaySubmits builds a second submit that writeDaySubmit refuses. Every check-out fails with no reason shown, and closeForgottenDays fails silently for ever.
2. The midnight boundary and the next-start close run the automatic check-out without the open-call or open-edit check. The manual check-out after midnight cannot happen at all, because AttendanceFlow ignores any press before checkout_earliest_time.
3. The refused-submit message tells the rep to sell and collect, but after a check-out the check-in gate refuses all of that.
4. The forgotten-day check-out has no explicit 'no check-out' marker, so today's server stores it as a real check-out days late. Days with no captures now raise CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT on legitimate reps. Both wait on the unbuilt server request.

Read from code only. Nothing was run, no test was executed, and nothing was walked on a device. The server side was read, not exercised: the 7-day backdate window, checkout_too_early (instant-based, so after midnight is fine), the settle per user-date, and CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT. I did not check: the AMO's open-call restore, the SubmitGate of a manual re-submit after check-out, the Room transaction-dispatcher behaviour on a real device, or the LocalPurge timing against the forgotten-day close.

### BLOCKING upheld=3/3: Check-out (and the forgotten-day close) fails every time once a manual Sales Submit stands on a route-day that the zone's Final Submit has locked

### BLOCKING upheld=3/3: The forgotten-day check-out reaches the server as an ordinary check-out, and empty days now raise CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT
WHERE: SrDay/AmoDay.closeForgottenDays (only the fix status marks it); backend/sync/.../DayStates.kt supervisorDay `checked_out_at = GREATEST(checked_out_at, :at)`; IngestService.kt checkoutWithoutSubmit (lines ~725-765); AutoDaySubmits writes no submit for a day with no captures
WHY: No field marks the record as 'closed by the phone, no check-out'. Today's server stores it as a genuine check-out. For an AMO it sets supervisor_day.checked_out_at to the capture time, which is the next start, days later. A real check-out with GPS off also carries provider_unavailable. Separately, the phone used to require a Sales Submit before check-out. Now a checked-in day with no routed captures sends a check-out with no day_submit, and the server raises the CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT risk signal (weight 20) on a legitimate rep. Both depend on the server request in docs/requests/android-gates.md s3, which is not built.
SCENARIO: An AMO checks in Monday, forgets to check out, and opens the app Thursday at 09:10. The server records Monday's check-out at Thursday 09:10, so the attendance and hours reports are wrong, and nothing is flagged. LOWS:
- The one-transaction claim holds: recordCheckOut wraps buildSubmits, writeDaySubmit, insertFix, insertAttendance and the outbox insert in one db.withTransaction. A kill between any two statements leaves all or nothing. sale.prepare() runs before it and writes nothing. The DPC, breadcrumb and sync hooks run after the commit. A kill after the commit but before onCheckOutCommitted leaves the apps held until reevaluateBlocking runs (this was already so before the slice). Concurrent closeForgottenDays calls (LaunchedEffect plus ON_RESUME at start) are made safe by the in-transaction 'none check_out' check.
- pending_count: inside recordCheckOut each submit's `unsentOn(date)` also counts the submits already inserted in the same loop, so with two routes the second submit's pending_count is one too high. RoomDaySubmitWriter reads unsentOn outside its own transaction (a small race). The server does not use pending_count in settle, so no figure is misjudged.
- device_counts are taken once, before any submit is inserted, and both route submits carry the date-wide counts. That matches the server's settle, which compares per user-date. It does not count the check_out (have >= want still holds).
- A quarantined day_submit stops 'standing' (RouteDayLock.standing). A quarantine can later be released on review, or for a device hold on a resend, after the rep has sold more and sent cycle 2. The server takes the newest cycle, so no figure is doubled, but rows captured after cycle 1 are then 'after submit' on a submit the server accepts. A retry_exhausted submit that is 'sent again' stands again next to a cycle 2.
- SR captures with route_id NULL (stock saved before a route is chosen, routeless dues) are not in captureRoutesOn, so a day with only those gets no submit. The server then raises CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT.
- Phone off for N days: the server backdate window is cfg.sync.max_backdate_days (default 7, calendar or working days, IngestService line ~899). Three days is accepted. More than 7 quarantines the whole day including its submit and check-out (business_date_out_of_window), and that quarantined submit then unlocks the phone. LocalPurge with cfg.app.local_history_days as low as 1 could purge D's acked capture rows before the forgotten-day close. The auto submit would then carry understated money and counts, or the check-in itself could be purged so the day is never closed. Unlikely with the default of 7.
- Logout while checked in: LogoutFlow lets the SR and AMO through with wipe = false, with no warning and no check-out. The day closes only when the same user logs in again on the same phone. On a shared phone, or if the rep moves to another phone, D never gets its submit or check-out, and no server-side midnight close exists (stated in the lane's notes).
- The OPEN_EDIT detection reads the in-memory sale flow (draft.edit with committed == null). An edit is not restored after a kill, which is harmless here. AMO restore of an open call into visitSession was not checked.
- A race at midnight: a check-out pressed at 23:59:5x whose fix read ends after 00:00 runs alongside the boundary auto-close. The auto-close wins and the manual one returns Failed with no message. The location reading is lost.
- If a stale day closes after today's check-in (a race, or a close that failed earlier then succeeds), BlockingEngine.onCheckOut sets checkedOut = true on TODAY's DPC state. That releases today's attendance-held apps.

## outbox-hold verdict=fail
SUMMARY: Slice 3's outbox hold (OutboxDao.nextSendable) works as intended for the normal case. The day_submit is never sent while an earlier record of its date is pending or in flight, and the check-out never while the submit is. They never share a batch, so nothing relies on the server's in-batch order. The hold is per date, and a kill loses nothing. retry_exhausted counts as final, so the hold releases and never traps the day. The cost is a sticky submit_count_mismatch, and the row arrives late if it is sent again. Two findings block. (1) A refused, quarantined or retry_exhausted automatic submit releases the check-out, and the server raises CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT against the rep. Nothing withdraws it, which contradicts the lane's own claim, and an integrity-quarantined submit can even arrive after the check-out. (2) The forgotten-day close also runs at Dhaka midnight and on every resume, bypassing the open-call guard. It closes the day under an open call (the visit_close and the sale are then refused) and replaces the rep's post-midnight check-out with one the phone makes without a location. The engine-level proof covers retry, final reject and kill only, not bisect, skip-ahead, exhausted, quarantine or the refused-submit path, and one of its assertions is always true. Nothing was run on a device or by Gradle; this is a read of commits 40731485b..74f588332 against the origin/lane/lead-docs-1022 rulings and the map.

### BLOCKING upheld=3/3: The check-out is released to the server when the automatic submit is refused, quarantined or retry_exhausted, so CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT is raised against the rep and never withdrawn
WHERE: android/core-database/src/main/kotlin/com/aktcl/aron/core/database/dao/OutboxDao.kt:44-45 (the check-out waits only while the submit is pending or in_flight); backend/sync/src/main/kotlin/com/aktcl/aron/backend/sync/IngestService.kt:719-762 (signal judged per check-out batch, ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, nothing clears it); android/core-database/src/main/kotlin/com/aktcl/aron/core/database/repo/RouteDayLock.kt:166-167; android/core-sync/src/main/kotlin/com/aktcl/aron/core/sync/SyncEngine.kt (isolate / releaseIntegrityQuarantine)
WHY: D-AGATE-06 says the outbox order means the server never sees 'a check-out without its submit'. The hold counts rejected, quarantined and retry_exhausted as final, so in each of those cases the check-out goes at once. The server has no effective day_submit in app.route_day_event for that user-day, so it stores a risk signal against the rep for a submit his phone made for him. The way out D-AGATE-06 offers (lock lifts, the rep submits cycle 2 from Sales Submit) arrives after the check-out, and the signal stays. The map's critic items 3 and 4 named exactly this. It asked for the two records to be tied into one family, or for the server to re-judge the signal; neither was done. Order can also invert: a day_submit quarantined with device_integrity_failed is final, so the check-out goes. releaseIntegrityQuarantine later sets the submit back to pending and resends it, so the server receives the submit after the check-out.
SCENARIO: The rep checks out at 17:30. The automatic day_submit answers HTTP 500 at single-family size on every run: isolate() counts one failure per run, and after rowMaxRetries=10 the submit is rejected(retry_exhausted). nextSendable now releases the check-out, which is accepted, and checkoutWithoutSubmit inserts CHECKOUT_WITHOUT_SUBMIT. The rep taps 'Send again' (requeueRetryExhausted) or makes a cycle-2 Sales Submit; that submit is stored, but the signal remains on the rep for that date. The same happens with any final server rejection of the auto submit (for example schema_invalid).

### BLOCKING upheld=2/3: The forgotten-day close also runs at Dhaka midnight and on every resume. It skips the open-call/edit guard and replaces the rep's own check-out after midnight with a phone-made one that has no location

- A predecessor quarantined with device_integrity_failed releases the submit (final). releaseIntegrityQuarantine later puts it back to pending and it is resent after the submit. This is unavoidable given the ruling, but it is not stated.
- The manual Sales Submit is unchanged: it is still online-only and needs zero pending or in-flight rows in the whole outbox, plus a server or digest count >= the device count. A held submit or check-out from yesterday therefore blocks today's manual submit until it drains; the check-out path remains the way forward. Its pending_count now reads unsentOn, which is about 0 under that gate. The behaviour is consistent but differs from the automatic path; no test of the manual path was loosened.
- Two dates pending at once are handled: the hold is per business_date, and seq order sends yesterday's tail first. Neither the hold query nor the engine relies on the server's in-batch order, because a submit can never share a batch with a pending or in-flight predecessor, nor a check-out with its submit. A kill has no 'release' step to lose: in-flight batches are resent first, as assembled.
- The 'real sync engine' proof (CheckOutOrderSyncTest) covers only a per-record retryable reject, a final server reject, a normal drain and a kill after an answered batch. Not covered through the engine: the 500 bisect or single-family isolate, 400 pointer isolation, skip-ahead (familySkipAfter=5, but the test retries twice), local retry_exhausted, quarantine and the integrity release, a refused submit releasing the check-out (blocking 1), two route-days, two dates, and Send again after the submit was sent. The bisect and exhausted cases are tested only at DAO level with hand-set states (CheckOutSubmitTest.kt:165-180).
