## resume-day-path verdict=fail
SUMMARY: The restore and 04:00 lens (rulings 1 to 5, SR and AMO) fails with three blocking findings. Nothing was run; this is a code reading of 2ca39b11..12a178bc, first-parent, and the merge 52cd8afe was not reviewed beyond the resolution files touched here.

Trace (a), kill at 23:50 and relaunch at 00:30: resumeDay brings the call back through callToRestoreAcross (today, then the open check-in dates). closeForgottenDays waits because a call is open, and the gate becomes EARLIER_CALL_OPEN. SR keeps VISIT/FORCE/NO_SALE open and the Sale tile leads only to that call (PICKER is gated). AMO keeps CONTROL_CALL/JOINT_CALL/SALE open. Every other gated screen names the earlier call and its outlet and offers Close.

Trace (b), relaunch at 08:00: three writes in order, each in its own transaction. First the visit_close dated D (abandoned, flagged by an end stamped on D+1). Then recordCheckOut writes D's day_submit(s) and the phone's check-out together. A kill between them is resumed cleanly with nothing written twice, as long as there is a single caller. The unsaved-draft drop really keeps saved memos. A half-entered QC or an unsaved edit of the call's memo is dropped with the notice.

Trace (f), ruling 5: sale, QC, close, collection, survey, content view and the AMO assessment, price and distribution records take the visit's date. A collection outside a call is today's and never attaches to an earlier visit.

Trace (g): check-in for D+1 works at any hour, including 00:10 after a midnight close, and stock for D+1 can then be entered.

Blocking:
1. A memo edit left open across midnight now traps the SR. The EDIT screen is gated by the new EARLIER_CALL_OPEN with no button that ends the edit. The 04:00 limit only ends calls, the day never closes, and check-in for D+1 is ignored.
2. The automatic 04:00 end records a call that has a saved sale or audit records as 'abandoned' with is_zero_sale=true. On AMO this goes round closeVisit's sold/audited classification, which the lane itself fixed. The server keeps the first close, so the wrong value cannot be corrected later.
3. The SR start and ON_RESUME paths run resumeDay at the same time with no mutex. The abandon can then write two visit_close rows for one visit; the server refuses the second as a content duplicate. The same race takes two location attempts for one closed day.

Other remaining ways to operate on D+1 without a D+1 check-in (lows):
- the open call itself before 04:00, which the ruling allows;
- that call kept in the foreground past 04:00, since no 04:00 timer exists;
- the clock set back after a reboot while offline (pre-existing).

What I could not check:
- runtime timing of the race;
- what the fix manager actually does in the background;
- whether drafts survive a process kill;
- the ruling-6 test replacement (outside this lens);
- the server's handling beyond RecordWriter.closeVisit.

LOWS:
- Rollover notice is memory-only (SrDay.kt:487, AmoDay.kt:284 MutableStateFlow). If the midnight timer closes D while the app is in the background and the OS kills the process before the rep opens it, he is never told the phone closed his day (ruling 2: 'the rep is told'). - The one location attempt runs inline in the start path. On AMO, MainActivity.kt:192-195 awaits d.resumeDay() before `day = d`, so the whole AMO shell waits up to the fix limit (timeoutS capped at 30 s, FixPorts.kt:89, plus the grace) once per forgotten day. On SR (SrApp.kt:280) it delays taskBoard.load, the permissions redirect and the bundle download. Missing permission or location off returns at once (FixManager.kt:56-59), so it is no trap. At the midnight timer the attempt runs even with the app in the background, where it usually gets no fix.

After a reboot and offline, a rep who sets the clock forward forces the abandon and close early (he harms only himself, and it is visible through clock_offset_ms=null). An SR who keeps the open call's sale screen in the foreground from 23:50 into the morning can still sell at that one outlet dated D with no D+1 check-in. With only an AMO memo edit open (no call), CONTROL_CALL, JOINT_CALL and SALE stay open under EARLIER_CALL_OPEN, so a new call at another outlet can be started on screen and is then refused by the write layer (dated D+1, not checked in). AmoDay.stockMeta (AmoDay.kt:675) dates an in-call stock movement with the clock (the STOCK screen is gated under EARLIER_CALL_OPEN, so it is not reachable there today).

- Tests: SrMidnightRestoreTest/AmoMidnightRestoreTest go through resumeDay with a moved trusted clock and calls read from Room, which is the production path. ## hold-and-rest verdict=fail
SUMMARY: Verdict: fail. I read lane/android-gates at 12a178bc (first-parent delta 2ca39b11..12a178bc) against D-LEAD-PREREQ-04 on lane/lead-docs-1022 (de00a618). Nothing was built, run or walked on a device, and I could not check the lane's mutation-proof and green-gate claims.

Rulings 6 and 7 are implemented correctly in OutboxDao. A check-out is held while the newest submit (highest outbox seq) of any route-day of its date is pending, in flight, quarantined or given up by the phone. It is released when each route-day's newest submit is accepted or refused for good, or at once when the date has no submit. Send again keeps the seq; a cycle-2 submit becomes the newest. A day_submit no longer waits behind its own date's check-out but still waits behind its date's other earlier records. I found no deadlock or starvation of later days. The manual Sales Submit gate leaves out only held check-outs, and pending_count and device_counts are unchanged.

The cycle-2 deadlock is reproduced with real rows through the real engine. The replaced step-2 test is a correct update for ruling 6, not a loosening. The merge is clean: DECISIONS complete, Room 10 with its schema file untouched, no core-ui edit by the lane, strings in both languages.

Three blocking findings:
1. A day_submit still being retried after check-out (outcome RETRY) gets the 'refused for good' text. It tells the rep the submit was refused and the check-out goes on its own, while the check-out is in fact held (SalesSubmitScreen.kt:495).
2. The AMO Outlet, Tasks, SR Stock and Exceptions screens were un-gated, but they write. While an earlier day's call is open, their saves end in the generic failure text instead of naming the open call, so ruling 4 is not met there. The lane lists this as NOT DONE. The outlet writers also claim a photo before the save is refused (MainActivity.kt:318).
3. NOT DONE item N4, a concrete family: memo acked, a survey of the same visit refused for good, a memo line given up by the phone. Because rank stands in for parentage, Send again is withheld for the line, and the screen tells the rep to redo the sale, which writes it twice (RoomDayAdapters.kt:103 and requeueRetryExhausted). This is pre-existing and rare.

Lows:
- A given-up submit that is never sent again holds the check-out for good. The rep is shown Send again, and the server has neither record, so the two sides agree. But this is not stated in D-AGATE-14, and it switches off the reconciliation digest and keeps the background sync firing.
- A theoretical purge case can hold a check-out again after its accepted submit is purged.
- Engine-test gaps: the review's exact refused-then-cycle-2 scenario, 'older accepted, newer pending', quarantine release, bisect, skip-ahead.
- The Bangla wording of gift_locked reads as 'the gift is unavailable'.

Not checked:
- SR Outlet Menu writes while an earlier call is open.

### BLOCKING upheld=3/3: NOT DONE N4 (rank stands in for parentage) can strand an unsent memo line, and the screen then tells the rep to redo the sale
WHERE: android/feature-dayclose/src/main/kotlin/com/aktcl/aron/feature/dayclose/RoomDayAdapters.kt:103 (parentRefused) and the SQL guard in OutboxDao.requeueRetryExhausted (~line 249-253, `p.rank < outbox.rank`)
WHY: In the visit family, rank 1 holds the memo and also unrelated siblings: visit_close, content_view and survey_response (RecordMapping :310-325). A rank-1 sibling that the server refused for good therefore counts as an 'ancestor' of an exhausted memo_line or qc_line (rank 2), even though that line's real parent memo was acked. Send again is withheld, both in the UI (outcome DROPPED) and in SQL (the row is never re-queued). The DROPPED text says 'NOT on the server... if it was a sale, do it again from the Memo screen'. So such a family exists, and the result is data that cannot be sent plus an instruction that writes the sale twice. This is pre-existing and rare, but it is a trap left in the code by a named NOT DONE item.
SCENARIO: Visit V (rank 0) is acked and memo M (rank 1) is acked. A survey_response S (rank 1) of the same visit is rejected schema_invalid because the survey version was retired. memo_line L2 (rank 2) failed with server_error until it became rejected(retry_exhausted). parentRefused is true (S.rank 1 < L2.rank 2), so the entry shows DROPPED with no Send again, and requeueRetryExhausted would not move L2 anyway. LOWS:
- SQL semantics of CHECKOUT_HELD (OutboxDao.kt:344-351), read and found correct. The row must be an attendance_event that the attendance_event table calls a check_out. It is held when, for some route-day of its date (route told by day_submit.route_id, with `IS` so a null supervisor-day route matches), the submit with the highest outbox seq is pending, in_flight, quarantined, or rejected with last_code retry_exhausted. It is released when every route-day's highest-seq submit is acked or rejected with any other code, or when the date has no submit row. 'Newest' means outbox seq, not cycle or created_at. Send again (requeueRetryExhausted) keeps the seq, so that submit stays the newest and holds until it is final. A cycle-2 row gets a higher seq and becomes the newest. The day_submit hold now skips check-out rows of its own date and still waits for every other earlier pending or in-flight row of that date. No new deadlock found: rows and submits of D+1 have another business_date, and held rows are filtered in WHERE, so they never use up the LIMIT. Ruling 7 holds: one non-final route-day keeps the check-out held.
- Manual gate: RoomDaySource.outboxStates uses pendingNotHeld, which counts every pending row except a held check-out; in-flight rows are counted separately. A rep with real unsent rows is still blocked. pending_count (unsentOn) and device_counts (committedCounts) in the day_submit are unchanged.
