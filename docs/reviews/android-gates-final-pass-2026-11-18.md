## owner-rules-end-to-end verdict=pass
SUMMARY: Pass, with no blocking defect found. The three owner rules hold on every SR and AMO path I could trace, and the guard for each is in the write layer inside the Room transaction, not only in the UI. Nothing was built or run; this is a code read of the full diff 39db6c734..bc38c0ad9.

- **Rule 1 (no check-in, no operational work):** every stock, call, sale, edit, skip, due, survey, content, outlet request, cluster, task and AMO record write is refused without an open check-in on the record's own date, and after a check-out. Reprints, the close of an already open call and Sales Submit are exempt by design. The TSO app is untouched.
- **Rule 2 (no sale beyond stock):** the check runs in the saving transaction, in base units, counting sale lines and reward packs, and an edit only gets back the memo it replaces. I found no path to negative stock.
- **Rule 3 (check-out submits the sales):** the submit(s) and the check-out commit together offline, all or nothing, and are sent in order later. A refused submit releases the check-out and can be redone.

The main residual risk is a lock-out with no manual way out if the phone's automatic close of a forgotten day, or of an earlier open call, kept failing. I found no realistic trigger for either. The other lows are a mislabelled check-out at the midnight edge, a wrong slip flag on a forgotten day's submit, and the lane's own open items (no race test, mutation proofs not re-run, closed_by commit marked not yet checked).

## close-save-concurrency verdict=pass
SUMMARY: Read-only review of git diff 39db6c734..bc38c0ad9 (Android only, 133 files) through the concurrency and lifecycle lens. Nothing was built or run; all conclusions are from reading the code. No blocking defect found.

What I checked and the result:

- Double attendance record (rep's check-out racing the phone's close, two resumes at once): SAFE. CaptureRepository.recordCheckOut checks inside its Room transaction that the date has a check-in and no check-out, so the second writer fails and writes nothing, whatever its client UUID. There is no worker or second process: the only callers of resumeDay are composition effects (SrApp.kt 281/291/302/318, AMO MainActivity.kt 195/211/219/229); no android:process, no WorkManager close job.
- Kill between the close and the phone mark, or between the close and the automatic submit: SAFE. The day_submit(s), the fix, the check_out, its outbox row and the phone_mark are one db.withTransaction (recordCheckOut); the call close and its mark are one transaction too (recordVisitClose).
- Save racing the phone's 04:00 end of a call: SAFE in both orders. Save runs under closeLock then the flow lock (commitUnderCloseLock, SrSaleKit.kt 180 / AmoSaleKit.kt 152); closeCall/closeVisit take the same closeLock, read the memo and close. Save first: the close reads it as sold. Close first: SaleFlow.commit refuses a new sale with CallClosed before any write.
- Save racing the day close at midnight: SAFE. An open call or open edit holds the day (forgottenDays re-read under resumeLock just before the write); every operational write re-checks the check-in gate inside its own transaction, so a write that loses the race is refused with words, never half-stored.
- Memo edit at midnight: SAFE. dropUnsavedDraft and commit share the flow lock; a Save that wins keeps its memo and is not reported as dropped, a drop that wins leaves nothing to save.
- Retry after a landed commit whose print failed (follow-up 1): SAFE in the normal path (in-memory committed answer comes before the closed-call refusal; the 04:00 drop keeps a committed state). One rare gap is listed as a low.
- Stale draft after a phone close (follow-up 6): SAFE. SaleFlow.start discards a leftover non-edit draft of a closed call when an edit starts; a new call has a new visit UUID and so a new draft key.
- Deadlock / ANR: SAFE. Lock order is always resumeLock -> closeLock -> flow lock -> Room; nothing takes them the other way, no non-reentrant Mutex is re-entered (closeVisitLocked/abandonLocked are called with the lock held, AmoSaleKit.closeVisit takes none), all locks are suspending, and the location attempt runs outside resumeLock under fixLock.tryLock. The Attendance screen settles while a close is pending but check-in stays off until the earlier day is closed.
- Clock moved: a check-out stored for a date closes it whatever the clock order (AttendanceGate.refusal); an edit whose date the clock cannot carry is refused (ClockMovedBackException). Trusted time is server-anchored. Edge cases are in the lows.
- Battery: SAFE. One delay to the next boundary (SR: 04:00, check-out time, midnight; AMO: 04:00, midnight), no tick, no continuous GPS. The phone's close makes one FixManager.take, bounded by the configured timeout (5 to 30 s) plus 2 s grace; a timeout or error becomes a 'no reading' fix and the close still goes on.

Could not verify: that the tests pass (nothing built here); the real behaviour of Room's withTransaction under cancellation on a device; the server's handling of closed_by; FinalSubmitLock.callToRestoreAcross and OutboxDao.nextSendable were not read in depth for this lens.
